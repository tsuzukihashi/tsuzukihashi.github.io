Notes

数値についてAbout the numbers

AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額を使っていて、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とは一致しません。

AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts.
