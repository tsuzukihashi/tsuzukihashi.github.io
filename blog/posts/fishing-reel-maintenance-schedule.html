回数で近づく手入れと、日数で近づく手入れ

リールの手入れの時期は、1つの数え方では決まりません。オーバーホールや注油は、釣りに行った回数で近づきます。ラインの交換やドラグの点検は、使わなくても日数で近づきます。

どのくらいの頻度がいいかは、リールの種類や海か川か、行く回数で変わります。リールの説明書を見るか、買った釣具店で聞くのが確かです。

ReelKeep 釣具ケア管理

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

リールやロッドを登録して、釣りに行ったら釣行を記録します。手入れの項目ごとに、回数で数えるか日数で数えるかが決まっていて、あと何釣行、あと何日という形で残りが出ます。

最初から入っている目安はこうです。どれも周期と名前を自分で直せます。

  • 海で使ったあとの水洗いは毎回
  • ベアリングの注油は20釣行ごと
  • オーバーホールは50釣行ごと
  • ライン交換とドラグの点検は90日ごと
  • ギアのグリスアップとハンドルのガタ点検は180日ごと
  • サビの点検と全体の清掃は30日ごと

日数で数える項目は、3日前と前日と当日に知らせます。回数で数える項目は、近づいたら次の朝に1回知らせて、目安を超えたら済ませるまで毎朝知らせます。水洗いは1釣行ごとなので、釣行を記録すると時期を過ぎた扱いになり、洗うまで毎朝声をかけます。

手入れのたびに履歴が残って、費用も一緒に記録できます。ホーム画面のウィジェットで、次に手入れが要る釣具と残りの回数や日数も見られます。

無料だと釣具1台とケア項目3つまで登録できます。プレミアムで釣具も項目も無制限になり、全期間の履歴と釣行のログ、保管場所ごとの分け方が使えて、広告が消えます。iPhone専用です。

ほかのアプリと比べなかった理由

App Storeで釣具の管理アプリを探すと、持っているルアーやロッドの一覧、釣果の記録をするアプリは見つかりました。手入れの時期を数えて知らせるアプリは、2026年11月15日の時点では見つけられませんでした。

アプリを入れたくないなら

ラインの交換のように日数で決まるものは、iPhoneのリマインダーで繰り返しを設定すれば済みます。回数で決まるものは、釣行のたびに手帳やメモに正の字をつけて数える方法もあります。

Care that comes due by trips, and care that comes due by days

There isn't one way to count when your reel needs care. Overhauls and oiling come due by how many times you go fishing. Line changes and drag checks come due with time, even if the reel sits unused.

How often depends on the type of reel, whether you fish salt or fresh water, and how often you go. Check your reel's manual or ask the tackle shop where you bought it.

What ReelKeep does

I made this

ReelKeep: Fishing Reel Care

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Register your reels and rods, and log each fishing trip. Each care item is counted either by trips or by days, and the app shows what's left as so many trips or so many days.

These are the default intervals. You can change the interval and the name of every one.

  • Rinsing after saltwater use, every trip
  • Oiling the bearings, every 20 trips
  • Overhaul, every 50 trips
  • Line change and drag check, every 90 days
  • Greasing the gears and checking the handle for play, every 180 days
  • Checking for rust and cleaning the whole reel, every 30 days

For items counted by days, it notifies you 3 days before, the day before, and on the day. For items counted by trips, it sends one notification the next morning when one is getting close, and every morning once it's past due until you do it. Rinsing is set to every trip, so logging a trip makes it overdue, and it keeps reminding you every morning until you rinse.

Every time you do some care, it's added to the history, along with what it cost. A Home Screen widget shows which gear needs care next and how many trips or days are left.

The free version holds 1 piece of gear and 3 care items. Premium makes both unlimited, adds the full history, a trip log, and grouping by storage spot, and removes the ads. It's iPhone only.

Why there's no comparison

Searching the App Store, I found apps for listing your lures and rods and for logging catches. As of November 15, 2026, I couldn't find one that counts and reminds you when gear needs care.

If you'd rather not install an app

For things that go by days, like line changes, a repeating reminder in the iPhone Reminders app is enough. For things that go by trips, you can keep a tally in a notebook or a note every time you go fishing.

FAQ

Is it free?
It's free to download and covers 1 reel and 3 care items. For two or more, you need the paid Premium.
I only fish in fresh water.
When you register gear, three items are added to start with: rinsing after salt water, line change, and overhaul. If you only fish fresh water, delete the rinse item or lengthen its interval.
Should I do the overhaul myself?
The app only tells you when. For taking the reel apart and servicing it, talk to the manufacturer or a tackle shop.
