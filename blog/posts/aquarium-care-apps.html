僕が作ったアプリ

水槽管理・水替え通知 TankMinder

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

水替え、フィルター掃除、水質チェック、水草のトリミングなど、お世話の種類を選ぶと周期が最初から入ります。水替えなら7日ごと、フィルター掃除なら30日ごとです。周期は1日から90日までの中で選び直せます。

通知は3日前と前日と当日です。過ぎたら、やったと記録するまで毎日鳴ります。水質はpH、水温、アンモニア、亜硝酸、硝酸、GH、KHの7項目を、測ったものだけ入れればグラフになります。

無料だとお世話は3件まで登録できます。水槽1つで水替えとフィルター掃除と水質チェックを入れると、ちょうど3件です。プレミアムで無制限になり、広告が消えます。

水族館ログ - 水槽管理

Ko Hyunshik · App Storeの評価 22件

この中ではいちばん評価が多いアプリです。紙の飼育日誌をアプリにしたような作りで、水換えや餌やりのタスクとメモをカレンダーで残します。

水質はpHや水温などをグラフで見られて、水槽はいくつでも追加できます。クラウドへのバックアップもあります。お世話の通知があるかどうかは、説明文には書かれていませんでした。

アクアリウムノート - 水槽・水質記録

towa imamoto · App Storeの評価 12件

水質の記録がいちばん細かいアプリです。水温やpHのほかに硝酸塩や塩素まで14項目あって、使う項目だけを表示できます。生体を写真つきで登録したり、日記を残したりもできます。

水換えなどのタスクは繰り返し登録して通知でき、iPhoneのカレンダーにも追加できます。無料だと水槽2つまでで、水質グラフは基本の5項目と直近30日です。全部の項目や好きな間隔の繰り返しは、プレミアムに入っています。

水換えの頻度はどう決めるか

水換えの頻度は、水槽の大きさや魚の数、ろ過の強さで変わります。TankMinderで最初に入る7日ごとは、あくまで出発点です。

迷ったら、魚を買ったお店で自分の水槽の場合を聞くのが確かです。決めた間隔をアプリに入れておけば、あとは覚えておかなくて済みます。

海水の水槽でも使えますか？
TankMinderはお世話の種類と周期を自分で決めるので、淡水でも海水でも使えます。アクアリウムノートは水槽ごとに淡水か海水かを登録できます。

I made this

Aquarium Care - TankMinder

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Pick a type of care, such as a water change, filter cleaning, a water test, or trimming plants, and a cycle is filled in for you. Water changes start at every 7 days and filter cleaning at every 30 days. You can change the cycle to anything from 1 to 90 days.

It notifies you 3 days before, the day before, and on the day. If the day passes, it keeps notifying you every day until you log it. For water tests, you enter only what you measured out of 7 parameters, pH, temperature, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, GH, and KH, and they show up on a graph.

The free version holds up to 3 care tasks. One tank with water changes, filter cleaning, and water tests is exactly three. Premium makes it unlimited and removes the ads.

Aquarium Log - Tank Manager

Ko Hyunshik · 22 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This one has the most ratings of the three. It works like a paper aquarium journal turned into an app, keeping water changes, feedings, and notes on a calendar.

You can see pH, temperature, and other readings on a graph, and add as many tanks as you like. It also backs up to the cloud. The description doesn't say whether it sends care reminders.

Aquarium Note: Tank and Water Log

towa imamoto · 12 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This one has the most detailed water log. Besides temperature and pH, it covers 14 parameters including nitrate and chlorine, and you can show only the ones you use. You can also register your fish with photos and keep a diary.

You can set repeating tasks like water changes with notifications, and add them to your iPhone calendar. The free version allows 2 tanks, with graphs for 5 basic parameters over the last 30 days. All parameters and custom repeat intervals come with Premium.

How often to change the water

How often you should change the water depends on the size of the tank, how many fish you keep, and how strong your filtration is. The 7 days that TankMinder starts with is only a starting point.

If you're not sure, the shop where you bought your fish can tell you what suits your tank. Once you decide on an interval and put it in an app, you don't have to keep it in your head.

Which one to pick

  • If you want a journal of your tanks on a calendar, Aquarium Log
  • If you want to track up to 14 water parameters in detail, Aquarium Note
  • If you want a reminder every day until you do the care you missed, TankMinder

FAQ

Are they free?
All three are free to download. TankMinder's free version holds 3 care tasks, and Aquarium Note's holds 2 tanks.
Do they work for saltwater tanks?
TankMinder lets you set the type of care and the cycle yourself, so it works for either. Aquarium Note lets you set each tank as freshwater or saltwater.
I have more than one tank.
Aquarium Log says you can add as many tanks as you want. TankMinder counts care tasks rather than tanks, so with two or more tanks you'll usually need Premium.
