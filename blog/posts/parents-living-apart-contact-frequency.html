連絡の頻度は、親と決めていい

毎日の人もいれば、週に1回の人も、月に1回の人もいます。何日おきが正しいという数字はありません。

困るのは、決めた間隔がいつの間にか空いてしまうことです。前に電話したのがいつだったか分からなくなる。ここをアプリに任せると楽になります。

見守りリマインダー - みまもり

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

父、母、祖父母のように相手を登録して、それぞれに連絡の間隔を決めます。ホーム画面には、前に連絡してから何日たったかが出ます。間隔を過ぎた人はオレンジ色になります。

通知は、人ごとに決めた時刻に毎日届きます。間隔に合わせて止まるわけではないので、週1回の相手でも毎日声をかけてくるのは知っておいてください。

無料で登録できるのは2人までです。3人目からと、季節や思い出の話題を出してくれる機能はプレミアムです。iPhone専用で、Android版はありません。

KinCue：家族への連絡リマインダー

Yu-Chuan Tai · App Storeの評価 0件

今日誰に連絡するとよいかを一覧で出してくれます。電話やメッセージのあとにアプリへ戻って連絡したと押すと、次のリマインダーが自動で決まります。

ウィジェットとSiriにも対応しています。登録人数の制限解除や詳しい分析、Apple Watchの機能は有料のKinCue+に入っています。

アプリを入れたくないなら

iPhoneに最初から入っているリマインダーでもできます。母に電話と書いて、繰り返しを毎週にするだけです。

ただ、前に電話してから何日たったかは分かりません。予定より早く話した週も、同じ日に鳴ります。

どれを選ぶか

  • 電話をかけたら勝手に記録してほしい、無料で済ませたいなら、Warmthly
  • 今日誰に連絡するかを一覧で見たい、Apple Watchでも使いたいなら、KinCue
  • 毎日決まった時刻に声をかけてほしい、何日空いたかをひと目で見たいなら、みまもり

よくある質問

何日おきに連絡するのがいいですか？
決まった正解はありません。親の暮らし方や体のこともあるので、親と話して決めるのが確かです。3本とも、その間隔を人ごとに決められます。
親のスマホにも入れる必要がありますか？
要りません。3本とも、連絡する側が自分のiPhoneに入れて使うアプリです。
親の安否確認にも使えますか？
この3本は、自分が連絡を忘れないためのアプリです。親が無事かどうかを知らせてくれる仕組みはありません。安否確認が目的なら、そのためのサービスを探してください。
無料で使えますか？
3本とも無料で入れられます。Warmthlyは完全無料と書かれています。みまもりは2人まで無料で、KinCueは人数の制限を外すところが有料です。

Short answer

There's no correct number of days between calls. The quickest fix is an app that keeps track of the rhythm you and your parents agree on and nudges you when it slips. If you want calls logged automatically, try Warmthly. If you'd rather get a nudge at the same time every day, take a look at CheckInSenior, which I made.

The rhythm is yours to set

Some people call every day, some once a week, some once a month. There's no number of days that's right for everyone.

The real problem is when the gap quietly stretches and you can't remember when you last called. That part is easy to hand off to an app.

CheckInSenior - Family Check

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Add each person, like your dad, mom, or grandparents, and pick a contact interval for each. The home screen shows how many days it's been since you last got in touch, and anyone past their interval turns orange.

Reminders arrive every day at the time you set for each person. They don't pause to match the interval, so even someone you call weekly will get a daily nudge. Worth knowing before you set it up.

The free version handles up to 2 people. A third person and up, plus conversation topic ideas about the season or old memories, are Premium. It's iPhone only, with no Android version.

Warmthly: Keep in Touch

KYUNGHWAN KIM · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Set a pace per person, like weekly for your mom and monthly for an old friend. When it's time, it sends one quiet notification in the evening, and you can call or message with one tap from the app. The timer resets on its own.

It also shows the local time for people abroad. The description says it's completely free, with no ads and no account.

KinCue: Family Call Reminders

Yu-Chuan Tai · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

It lists who you should reach out to today. After a call or message, go back to the app and tap that you got in touch, and the next reminder is set automatically.

It works with widgets and Siri. Removing the limit on people, deeper stats, and Apple Watch features are part of the paid KinCue+.

If you'd rather not install an app

The Reminders app that comes with your iPhone can do this. Write call Mom and set it to repeat weekly.

What it can't tell you is how long it's been since you actually called. If you talked earlier than planned, it still goes off on the same day.

Which one to pick

  • If you want calls logged automatically and want it free, Warmthly
  • If you want a list of who to reach out to today, or want it on Apple Watch, KinCue
  • If you want a nudge at the same time every day and to see at a glance how many days it's been, CheckInSenior

FAQ

How many days should I leave between calls?
There's no set answer. Your parents' routines and health matter, so it's best to decide together. All three apps let you set the interval per person.
Do my parents need to install it too?
No. All three are apps you install on your own iPhone, as the one doing the calling.
Can I use them to check that my parents are safe?
These three are for remembering to get in touch yourself. None of them can tell you whether your parents are okay. If that's what you need, look for a service made for safety checks.
Are they free?
All three are free to download. Warmthly says it's completely free. CheckInSenior is free for up to 2 people, and KinCue charges to remove its limit on people.
