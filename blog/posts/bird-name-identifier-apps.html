BirdLens - 野鳥AI図鑑

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

鳥を撮るか、鳴き声を録音すると、AIが名前の候補を出します。体長や分類も一緒に出て、似ている候補も並びます。

無料で調べられるのは、写真と鳴き声を合わせて2回までです。生態や観察のコツ、似た鳥との見分け方はプレミアムで見られます。調べるときはネットにつながっている必要があります。

Merlin Bird ID by Cornell Lab

Cornell University · App Storeの評価 195件

Google Play版

コーネル大学鳥類学研究所が作っている無料のアプリです。写真、鳴き声の録音、いくつかの質問に答える方法、地域の鳥を探す方法の4つで鳥を調べられます。

地域ごとのバードパックを入れて使う仕組みで、日本のパックもあります。Google PlayにAndroid版もあります。

Picture Bird - 撮ったら、判る--1秒鳥図鑑

Next Vision Limited · App Storeの評価 2,751件

評価が2,751件あって、この中ではいちばん使われています。写真と鳴き声の両方に対応していて、説明文では1万種以上を識別できると書かれています。

鳥に向けてズームすると写真をくっきりさせるカメラや、餌やりのガイドもあります。年間のプレミアムがあって、7日間のお試しが付いています。

ききしる

Shuichi Kodama · App Storeの評価 2件

声を聞き取って名前を出すことに絞ったアプリです。対象は日本にすむ247種で、名前は和名と漢字と学名で出ます。迷うときは、たぶんと断って候補を出してくれます。

識別は全部端末の中で済んで、アカウントも広告もありません。Apple Watchだけでも聞き取りから名前まで出せます。

よくある質問

無料で使えますか？
4本とも無料で入れられます。Merlinは説明文で無料のアプリと書かれています。BirdLensは無料で2回まで調べられて、そのあとはプレミアムです。
姿が見えなくても調べられますか？
4本とも鳴き声から調べられます。ききしるは声を聞き分けることに絞ったアプリです。
電波の届かない山の中でも使えますか？
説明文で端末の中だけで識別すると書かれているのは、ききしるです。BirdLensは調べるときにネットが要ります。
Androidで使えるのはどれですか？
この4本の中では、MerlinがGoogle Playにもあります。BirdLensはiPhone専用です。

Short answer

You can name a bird with an app that suggests candidates from a photo or a recording of its song. If you want something solid for free, go with Merlin from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. If you only care about songs and want an app focused on birds in Japan, try Kikishiru. If you want both photo and song, plus tips on telling look-alikes apart, take a look at BirdLens, which I made.

I made this

BirdLens - Bird AI Guide

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Take a photo of a bird or record its song, and the AI suggests what it is. You also get its body length and classification, along with other likely candidates.

You can identify two birds for free, counting photos and recordings together. Ecology, birding tips, and how to tell similar birds apart come with Premium. You need an internet connection to identify a bird.

Merlin Bird ID by Cornell Lab

Cornell University · 195 App Store ratings

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

This is a free app from the Cornell Lab of Ornithology. You can identify a bird four ways, from a photo, from a recording, by answering a few simple questions, or by browsing the birds in your area.

It works with regional bird packs, and there is one for Japan. There is also an Android version on Google Play.

Picture Bird: Bird identifier

Next Vision Limited · 2,751 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

With 2,751 ratings, this is the most used of the four. It works with both photos and songs, and the description says it can identify more than 10,000 species.

It also has a camera that sharpens the shot as you zoom in on a bird, and a guide to feeding backyard birds. There is a yearly Premium plan with a 7-day trial.

Kikishiru

Shuichi Kodama · 2 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This app does one thing, it listens to a song and tells you the bird. It covers 247 species that live in Japan, and gives the Japanese name with its kanji and the scientific name. When it isn't sure, it says so and offers its best guesses.

Everything runs on the device, with no account and no ads. It even works on an Apple Watch by itself, from listening to showing the name.

Which one to pick

  • If you want to try both photos and songs without paying, Merlin
  • If you want the one most people already use, Picture Bird
  • If you want to name birds in Japan by their song while you walk, Kikishiru
  • If you want photo and song, plus how to tell look-alikes apart, BirdLens

FAQ

Are they free?
All four are free to download. Merlin describes itself as a free app. BirdLens lets you identify two birds for free, and after that it is Premium.
Can I identify a bird I can't see?
All four can identify a bird from its song. Kikishiru is built only for that.
Do they work in the mountains with no signal?
Kikishiru is the one whose description says identification runs entirely on the device. BirdLens needs the internet to identify a bird.
Which one works on Android?
Of these four, Merlin is also on Google Play. BirdLens is iPhone only.

BirdLens has no ratings yet. If it tells you the name of a bird singing in your local park, I'd love to hear about it 🐦
