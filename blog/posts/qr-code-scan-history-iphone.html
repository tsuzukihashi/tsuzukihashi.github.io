まず、iPhoneのカメラには履歴が無い

iPhoneのカメラでQRコードを読むと、出てきたリンクを開くだけで、読み取ったこと自体はどこにも残りません。履歴を探しても見つからないのはそのためです。

開いたページなら、Safariの履歴に残っています。Wi-Fiやテキストのように、ページを開かないQRコードは後から追えません。

これから読むQRコードを残したいなら、履歴を残すQRコードリーダーで読むのがいちばん早いです。

First, the iPhone Camera has no history

When you scan a QR code with the iPhone Camera, it just offers the link to open. The scan itself isn't saved anywhere, which is why you can't find a history.

If you opened a web page, it's in your Safari history. QR codes that don't open a page, like Wi-Fi details or plain text, can't be traced afterward.

If you want to keep the codes you scan from now on, the quickest fix is to scan them with a QR reader that keeps a history.

FAQ

Can I find a QR code I scanned with the Camera before?
The Camera has no history, so the code itself can't be found. If you opened a page, you can find it in your Safari history.
If I install one of these apps, will my older scans show up?
No. The history only includes codes you scan with that app.
Are they free?
All four are free to download. ikaQR and QR Code Reader: Quick Scan show ads, and have nothing to buy.
