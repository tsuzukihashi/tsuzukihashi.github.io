まずはiPhoneのリマインダーで足りるか

iPhoneに最初から入っているリマインダーは無料で、日時の通知、繰り返し、リストを作るところまで全部できます。正直、たいていの人はこれで足ります。

別のアプリを探すのは、標準のアプリの項目が多くて迷う人か、標準にない繰り返し方がほしい人です。

リマインダー 記憶を呼び起こす

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

入れるのはタイトルと日時だけです。決めた時刻に通知が届いて、それ以外の機能はありません。リストもタグもメモ欄もないので、迷うところがないアプリです。

そのぶん、できることは標準のリマインダーより少ないです。無料で作れるのは3件までで、終わったものも3件に数えるので、済んだものは消して使います。毎日、毎週、毎月の繰り返しはプレミアムです。

Memento: シンプルなリマインダー

Mark DiFranco · App Storeの評価 185件

明日9時に水道屋さんに電話、のように書くだけで、通知の日時やリストが決まります。iPhoneのリマインダーの中身をそのまま使うので、標準のアプリから乗り換えても続きから使えます。

iPhone、iPad、Mac、Apple Watchで同期できて、ウィジェットやSiriにも対応しています。有料のアプリで、2016年から1人の個人開発者さんが作り続けていると書かれていました。

つげるくん - リマインダー付ToDoリスト

Takayuki Yuda · App Storeの評価 199件

強いのは繰り返しの細かさです。毎週同じ曜日のほかに、毎月第1週だけ、6月と12月だけ、のような決め方ができます。

マンガや雑誌の発売日、決まった時期の作業のように、標準のアプリでは繰り返しにしにくいものに向いています。サウンドで知らせる機能もあります。

予定通知リスト リマインダー シンプルにやることをお知らせ

First, check whether your iPhone's Reminders is enough

The Reminders app that comes with your iPhone is free, and handles timed notifications, repeats, and lists. Honestly, it's enough for most people.

It's worth looking elsewhere if the built-in app feels like too many options, or if you need a kind of repeat it doesn't offer.

Where they differ is how much has been stripped away, and how detailed the repeats get.

I made this

BaseReminder

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

All you enter is a title and a time. A notification arrives at that time, and that's it. There are no lists, tags, or note fields, so there's nothing to get lost in.

That also means it does less than the built-in Reminders. The free version holds 3 reminders, and finished ones still count toward that, so you delete them as you go. Daily, weekly, and monthly repeats are Premium.

Memento - Better Reminders

Mark DiFranco · 185 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Just write something like call the plumber tomorrow at 9, and it sets the time and list for you. It works on top of your existing iPhone Reminders, so switching over picks up right where you left off.

It syncs across iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch, and works with widgets and Siri. It's a paid app, and the description says one independent developer has been making it since 2016.

TodoNoticer - Todo, Schedule

Takayuki Yuda · 199 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Its strength is detailed repeats. Besides the same weekday every week, you can set things like only the first week of every month, or only in June and December.

It suits things that are awkward to repeat in the built-in app, like manga or magazine release days or seasonal chores. It can alert you with sound too.

Reminder List Easy Day Planner

NilCraft Inc. · 16 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

You enter what and when, and that's it. It has advance alerts that go off a bit before the time, handy when you need to get ready for a meeting.

You can switch between a list and a calendar, and pick the notification sound.

Which one to pick

  • If you want everything for free, the built-in iPhone Reminders
  • If you want a faster way to use your iPhone Reminders, including on a Mac, Memento
  • If you need detailed repeats like the first week of every month, TodoNoticer
  • If you want an alert a little before the time too, Reminder List Easy Day Planner
  • If you want the fewest steps possible, just a title and a time, BaseReminder

FAQ

How are these different from the built-in Reminders?
The built-in app does more. These four either cut features down to fewer steps or add repeats and input methods the built-in app doesn't have.
Are they free?
Memento is a paid app. BaseReminder is free for up to 3 reminders, and the other two don't mention pricing in their descriptions.
Can they remind me based on location?
Of these four, Memento supports location-based reminders. The built-in iPhone Reminders can do it too.
