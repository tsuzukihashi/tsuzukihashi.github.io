分析Analysis

1,050,092円。月収100万円を超えました。1000万円への道のりの1割に、一瞬だけ手が届いた月です。

立役者は推しアイランドです。SNSでバズってダウンロードが36,700件まで跳ね、約3,000円の買い切りプランが217本売れました。買い切りの強さは、バズの熱をその場で現金にできることです。サブスクなら初月分しか入らない熱量が、一撃で入ってくる。

AdMobも205,320円で過去最高でした。ただ冷静に見れば、このうち55万円はバズという花火です。花火は必ず消える。消えたあとに何が残るかは、来月の数字が教えてくれます。

$6,584. My monthly revenue went over ¥1 million (about $6,270). For a moment, I reached a tenth of the way to ¥10 million.

The hero was Oshi Island. It went viral on social media, downloads jumped to 36,700, and the one-time purchase plan at about $19 sold 217 copies. The strength of a one-time purchase is that it turns the heat of a viral moment into cash on the spot. With a subscription, only the first month of that excitement would come in. Here it comes in all at once.

AdMob also hit a record at $1,287. But looking at it calmly, $3,448 of this month is a firework called a viral hit. Fireworks always fade. Next month's numbers will tell me what's left after this one does.

NextAction

この数字を見て、来月やることを3つ決めました。Based on these numbers, I picked three things to do next month.

  1. 反動減に備えて、推しアイランドに週間プランの継続価値を残すTo brace for the drop, keep giving Oshi Island's weekly plan a reason to stay subscribed
  2. うんちくんをバズに頼らないストック収益として育て続けるKeep growing UNCHIKUN as stock revenue that doesn't rely on going viral
  3. 配信量が増えたのでAdMobのフォーマット別eCPMを見直すAd volume has grown, so review AdMob eCPM by format

100万円は通過点。でも、この景色は忘れずにいたい。¥1 million (about $6,270) is just a stop along the way. But I want to remember this view.

それでは、また来月の報告で。See you in next month's report.

数値について。AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額で、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。3つとも暦月の1日から末日で区切っています。App Store Connectの財務報告はAppleの会計月で締めるので、同じ月でも対象期間がずれ、画面に出る金額とは一致しません。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とも一致しません。
About the numbers. AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. All three are cut by calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. App Store Connect's financial reports close on Apple's fiscal months, so the period covered shifts even for the same month, and the amounts won't match what's shown there. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts either. Dollar amounts are converted from yen at the ECB month-end rate (¥159.50 per US dollar on 2026-03-31), and every figure in this issue, including last month's, uses that same rate.
