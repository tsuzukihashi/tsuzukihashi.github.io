セッション詳細（fortee.jp）

ダンボー田中さんの発表はとても場慣れしている感があって、自然体ですごいなぁって思いました(コナミ感)
テストのデッドロック問題など、実際に自分も遭遇したらどうしようと思える問題を取り上げていました。
問題に対するアプローチを、適切に言語化していて自分も言語化する能力を鍛えないといけないと、改めて思いました。

また、髪の毛が金色でかっこよかったです。

まとめ

いろいろと難しいし、自分の勉強不足を痛感しましたが、いつもお世話になってるiOSエンジニアの方々に出会えて最高でした！
来年も、参加しようと思います！
また、自分もいつかスピーカーとして参加できるように、活動をしていきたいです。

3年後の2022年、実際にiOSDC Japanでスピーカーとして登壇できました！夢が叶いました。

I went to iOSDC 2019 for the first time!

I attended both days, Friday 9/6 and Saturday 9/7, 2019, as a regular attendee.

The opening had so much energy that I was completely overwhelmed...

The Evangelion-style opening got me so hyped!

A complete guide to how importing and linking libraries works

Session details (fortee.jp)

The content was pretty hard and I didn't fully understand it, so I decided to try building a library myself.

Swift Clean Code Adventure

Session details (fortee.jp)

The talk was really careful and thorough, and it taught me a lot about how to present ><
I use Clean Architecture day to day, so how they applied the architecture was helpful too.

I couldn't find a safe way to rewrite a Fat ViewController, so I thought about which pain to accept

Session details (fortee.jp)

Danbo Tanaka-san seemed totally at home on stage, and I thought it was amazing how natural he was (deep thoughts, I know)
He took on problems like deadlocks in tests, the kind that make you think "what would I do if I ran into that?"
He put his approach to each problem into words so precisely that it reminded me I need to train my own ability to put things into words.

Also, his gold hair looked really cool.

Day 2

Can an iOS app have "consciousness"? Artificial intelligence (AI) beyond deep learning

Session details (fortee.jp)

Related session (fortee.jp)

The path from neuroscience all the way to deep learning was fascinating.
It laid out concrete challenges standing between apps and consciousness, and I got so excited!

Now is the time for watchOS

The room was packed, with people standing as far back as they could fit. It was hugely popular!
It walked through watchOS in detail, from the very first version to the latest, and I learned a lot.
I'm definitely buying an Apple Watch and building for it!

Wrapping up

A lot of it was hard, and I felt painfully how much I still have to learn, but getting to meet the iOS engineers I always rely on was the best!
I plan to go again next year!
And I want to keep working so that someday I can take part as a speaker myself.

Three years later, in 2022, I actually got to speak at iOSDC Japan! My dream came true.
