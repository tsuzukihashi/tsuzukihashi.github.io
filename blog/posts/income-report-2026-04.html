分析Analysis

359,991円。3月の100万円から65.7%減りましたが、これは織り込み済みの反動です。バズ前の2月と比べれば67%増えていて、地力は確実に上がっています。

今月はっきりしたのは花火とストックの違いです。推しアイランドの買い切りは217本から3本まで消えました。一方で週間プラン299件は継続収益として残った。先月、継続課金の価値を残すと書いた施策が効いています。

そしてうんちくんが月17万円の柱に育ちました。バズに頼らず月3万ダウンロードを積み、サブスクと広告の両輪で稼ぐ。この形が理想です。

$2,299. Down 65.7% from March's ¥1 million (about $6,387), but I had already priced in that drop. Compared with February, before the viral hit, it's up 67%, so the baseline has definitely risen.

What became clear this month is the difference between fireworks and stock. Oshi Island's one-time purchases went from 217 to 3. Meanwhile, 299 weekly plans stayed on as recurring revenue. The move I wrote about last month, keeping the value of the recurring plan, is working.

And UNCHIKUN grew into a $1,086-a-month pillar. It stacks up 30,000 downloads a month without relying on virality and earns from subscriptions and ads together. That's the ideal shape.

NextAction

この数字を見て、来月やることを3つ決めました。Based on these numbers, I picked three things to do next month.

  1. うんちくんにAI機能を足して、単価と継続率を上げるAdd AI features to UNCHIKUN to raise revenue per user and retention
  2. うんちくんPlay版の課金プランを増やすAdd more paid plans to UNCHIKUN on Play
  3. 月30万円台を下限として固定できるか検証するTest whether I can lock in the $1,916 range as the floor

バズは水物、ストックは資産。両方追います。Viral hits come and go. Stock is an asset. I'm going after both.

数値について。AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額で、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。3つとも暦月の1日から末日で区切っています。App Store Connectの財務報告はAppleの会計月で締めるので、同じ月でも対象期間がずれ、画面に出る金額とは一致しません。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とも一致しません。
About the numbers. AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. All three are cut by calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. App Store Connect's financial reports close on Apple's fiscal months, so the period covered shifts even for the same month, and the amounts won't match what's shown there. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts either. Dollar amounts are converted from yen at the ECB month-end rate (¥156.56 per US dollar on 2026-04-30), and every figure in this issue, including last month's, uses that same rate.
