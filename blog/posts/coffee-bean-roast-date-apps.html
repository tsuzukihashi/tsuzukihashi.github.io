珈琲鮮度・焙煎日管理 BeanFresh

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

焙煎日を入れると、ガス抜き、飲み頃、飲みきりどきの順に今どこにいるかをメーターで出します。最初に入るのはガス抜き4日、飲み頃は焙煎日から28日までです。ガス抜きは2日から7日、飲み頃の長さは14日から42日の中で、豆に合わせて選び直せます。

通知は、飲み頃に入る前日と当日、飲みきりの3日前と当日です。過ぎたら、飲みきったと記録するまで毎日鳴ります。淹れ方や豆と湯の量、挽き目を残す抽出の記録もあります。

無料だと豆は3件まで登録できます。プレミアムで無制限になり、広告が消えます。冷凍した豆の扱いはありません。

Mame Log｜コーヒー豆の飲み頃・鮮度管理

Uetake Kouya · App Storeの評価 1件

焙煎日か開封日を入れると、浅煎り、中煎り、深煎りごとに飲み頃を自動で計算します。日数を自分で考えたくない人には、こちらのほうが楽ですね。

BeanFreshに無いのが冷凍の扱いです。常温と冷凍を分けて管理できて、冷凍している間は経過が止まります。豆カレンダーで、いつどの豆を飲んでいたかも振り返れます。無料で、広告が出ます。

アプリを入れなくてもいい人

豆を1袋ずつしか買わないなら、袋に書いてある焙煎日を見て数えれば足ります。アプリが役に立つのは、何袋かを並行して開けていて、どれから飲むか迷う人です。

どちらを選ぶか

  • 豆を冷凍して保存する人や、焙煎度で自動に決めてほしい人は、Mame Log
  • 日数を豆ごとに決めて、飲みきるまで知らせてほしい人や、淹れ方も記録したい人は、BeanFresh

よくある質問

焙煎日から何日が飲み頃ですか？
豆や焙煎度、保存の仕方で変わります。買ったお店で聞いた日数をアプリに入れておくのが確かです。BeanFreshは最初に4日目から28日目までが入っています。
無料で使えますか？
どちらも無料で入れられます。BeanFreshは無料だと豆3件まで、Mame Logは無料で広告が出ます。
焙煎日が書いていない豆はどうしますか？
Mame Logは開封日から数えることもできます。BeanFreshは焙煎日から数えるので、分からなければ買った日を入れて目安にしてください。

Short answer

The best window depends on the beans and the roast, so the shop where you bought them is the best place to ask. The counting itself you can leave to an app. If you freeze your beans or want the window set by roast level automatically, try Mame Log. If you want to set the resting and drink-by days yourself and also log your brews, BeanFresh, which I made, is a good fit.

BeanFresh - Coffee Freshness

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Enter the roast date and a meter shows where the beans are now, from resting to their best days to time to finish them. It starts with 4 days of resting and a window that lasts until 28 days after roasting. You can change the resting time to anything from 2 to 7 days, and the window from 14 to 42 days, to suit the beans.

It notifies you the day before the window opens, the day it opens, 3 days before the drink-by day, and on that day. After that, it reminds you every day until you log that the bag is finished. There's also a brew log for your method, the amount of beans and water, and the grind.

The free version holds up to 3 bags of beans. Premium makes it unlimited and removes the ads. It doesn't handle frozen beans.

Mame Log: Coffee Bean Freshness

Uetake Kouya · 1 App Store rating

View on the App Store

Enter the roast date or the day you opened the bag, and it calculates the window automatically for light, medium, or dark roasts. If you'd rather not think about the number of days, this one is easier.

What BeanFresh doesn't have is freezer support. It tracks room-temperature and frozen beans separately, and pauses the clock while they're frozen. A bean calendar also shows which beans you were drinking and when. It's free, with ads.

When you don't need an app

If you only buy one bag at a time, checking the roast date printed on the bag is enough. An app helps when you have several bags open at once and can't decide which to drink first.

Which one to pick

  • If you freeze your beans or want the window set automatically by roast level, Mame Log
  • If you want to set the days for each bag, get reminders until it's finished, and log your brews, BeanFresh

FAQ

How many days after roasting are beans at their best?
It depends on the beans, the roast, and how you store them. The safest bet is to ask the shop and put those days into the app. BeanFresh starts with day 4 through day 28.
Are they free?
Both are free to download. BeanFresh holds 3 bags for free, and Mame Log is free with ads.
What if the bag doesn't show a roast date?
Mame Log can also count from the day you opened the bag. BeanFresh counts from the roast date, so if you don't know it, enter the day you bought the beans as a rough guide.
