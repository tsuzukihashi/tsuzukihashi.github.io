僕が作ったアプリ

猫背改善と姿勢リマインダー：猫背は猫だけで充分にゃ。

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 9件

Google Play版

猫を1匹選ぶと、決めた時間帯に、決めた間隔でその猫から通知が届きます。顎を引くにゃ、のように、座ったままできる動きをひとこと添えてきます。

通知から完了を押すと記録になって、10分後にもう一度知らせてもらうこともできます。姿勢を直した回数と、続いた日数が残ります。

無料だと間隔は30分か60分で、記録は1日5回、10分後の通知は1日3回までです。猫は12匹いて、無料で仲間にできるのは4匹です。15分、45分、90分の間隔、平日だけの設定、残りの8匹はプレミアムです。Google PlayにAndroid版もあります。

猫背にゃおし隊 | 通知で姿勢改善の習慣リマインダー

Romiick LLC · App Storeの評価 3件

通知が来たら背筋を伸ばす、それだけのシンプルなアプリです。警備猫が見回りに来るという設定で、通知が届きます。

捕まった猫を保釈したり、警備ランクを上げたりする遊びの機能があります。どこが有料かは説明文に書かれていませんでした。

猫背改善アプリ 背筋がシュッとニャン太くん

TSUKUTTEMITA, K.K. · App Storeの評価 13件

スマホを胸ポケットに入れておくと、猫背になったときに猫の鳴き声で教えてくれます。時間ではなく、姿勢そのものに反応するのがほかと違うところです。

胸ポケットのある服が要ります。アプリを開いたままでないと検知できないと書かれています。すべての機能を使うには課金が必要です。

姿勢改善 - スマホ首・猫背防止

yuta tabane · App Storeの評価 5件

ピクチャ・イン・ピクチャで小さな窓を画面に浮かべて、スマホをのぞき込むと赤、目の高さで持つと緑に変わります。ほかのアプリを使いながら色を見るだけで済みます。

見ているのはスマホの持ち方なので、机でパソコンに向かっているときの姿勢は分かりません。テーマカラーは2種類が無料です。

アプリを入れたくないなら

iPhoneの時計アプリのタイマーや、リマインダーの繰り返しでもできます。1時間ごとに鳴らして、鳴ったら座り直すだけです。

ただ、毎回同じ音だと、だんだん聞き流すようになります。言うことが毎回変わるのは、専用のアプリのいいところですね。

Posture Reminder: Cat Coach

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 9 App Store ratings

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

Pick a cat, and it sends you reminders at the interval you choose during the hours you set. Each one comes with a quick line from the cat, like telling you to tuck your chin, something you can do without getting up.

Tap done on the notification to log it, or ask it to remind you again in 10 minutes. It keeps track of how many times you straightened up and how many days in a row.

On the free version you can choose 30 or 60 minute intervals, log up to 5 times a day, and snooze up to 3 times a day. There are 12 cats, and 4 of them are available for free. 15, 45, and 90 minute intervals, a weekdays-only setting, and the other 8 cats are Premium. There's also an Android version on Google Play.

Posture Patrol

Romiick LLC · 3 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

A simple app built around one thing. When the notification comes, sit up straight. The idea is that security cats come by on patrol to check on your spine.

There are playful extras, like bailing out cats arrested for slouching and ranking up your patrol. The description doesn't say which parts are paid.

Nyanta: The Cat That Meows When You Slouch

TSUKUTTEMITA, K.K. · 13 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Put your phone in your shirt's chest pocket, and it meows when you start to slouch. It reacts to your actual posture rather than a timer, which sets it apart.

You'll need a shirt with a chest pocket, and the description says it only detects slouching while the app is open. Using every feature requires a purchase.

Neck Saver

yuta tabane · 5 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

It floats a small window on your screen using picture-in-picture. It turns red when you hunch over your phone and green when you hold it at eye level, so you only need to glance at the color while using other apps.

Because it watches how you hold your phone, it can't tell how you're sitting at a computer. Two color themes are free.

If you'd rather not install an app

A timer in the iPhone Clock app or a repeating reminder works too. Have it go off every hour and sit up when it does.

The catch is that hearing the same sound every time, you start to tune it out. Getting a different message each time is where the dedicated apps help.

Which one to pick

  • If it's the angle of your neck while looking at your phone, Neck Saver
  • If you want it to react to your actual posture, Nyanta
  • If you want a simple sit-up-straight reminder with a playful twist, Posture Patrol
  • If you want reminders on a schedule while you work, or you also use Android, Cat Coach

FAQ

Can an app fix my posture?
All four are there to help you notice. I can't say they'll fix anything. If your back or shoulders hurt, please see a doctor.
Are they free?
All four are free to download. Nyanta says a purchase is needed to use every feature.
Which one works on Android?
Of these four, Cat Coach is the one I could confirm has an Android version.
