分析Analysis

464,476円。前月から6.1%減りました。それでも3月と8月に次いで3番目に高い月です。

下がった分はほぼ広告です。AdMobが155,880円から128,577円へ、27,303円減りました。月全体の減少は30,412円なので、9割が広告で説明できます。中身はうんちくんのiOS版で、116,051円から96,992円。表示回数が752,203回から701,335回へ減り、eCPMも154円から138円に下がりました。8月号で単価は上がったと書いたばかりですが、7月の148円よりも下まで落ちました。

うんちくんの新規ダウンロードは22,445件から16,410件。8月号のNextActionに母数を戻すと書いて、戻せませんでした。2か月で半分です。ところが課金は77,664円から87,508円に増えていて、年額プランが21,542円から39,736円へ倍近くになりました。Playの課金も4,087円から18,492円です。広告と課金とPlayを足すと215,143円で、8月とほとんど変わりません。入ってくる人は減り続けているのに、残った人が払ってくれている状態です。これがいつまで持つかは分かりません。

旅行思い出マップは課金が108,094円で、先月に続いて自己最高です。新規ダウンロードは減ったのに、1年間サポータープランは44,879円から49,130円に増えました。

あなたレシートは逆の動きでした。新規ダウンロードは1,985件から3,147件へ6割増えたのに、売上は8月の94,640円から58,238円に落ちています。3,000円の買い切りが半分近くまで減り、代わりに週額プランが伸びました。ダウンロード1件あたりのApp Store売上は38.9円から15.3円です。

買い切りはほかのアプリでも売れていて、HomeKeep、TankMinder、WatchKeepがそれぞれ2,500円台で1本ずつ。8月に出した7本は、9月の新規ダウンロードが合わせて181件、売上はEyeMoleとnokkiの949円でした。

Google Playは7月から9,356円、22,282円、30,070円と毎月増えています。9月はうんちくんの月額プランが18,492円で、Playでもうんちくんが一番になりました。

$2,958. Down 6.1% from last month. Even so, it's my third-highest month after March and August.

Almost all of the drop is ads. AdMob fell from $993 to $819, down $174. The month as a whole fell by $194, so ads explain 90% of it. Specifically, it's UNCHIKUN on iOS, which went from $739 to $618. Impressions fell from 752,203 to 701,335, and eCPM dropped from $0.98 to $0.88. I had just written in the August issue that the rate went up, and now it has fallen below July's $0.94.

UNCHIKUN's new downloads went from 22,445 to 16,410. In August's NextAction I wrote that I'd bring the volume back, and I couldn't. It has halved in two months. Purchases, though, rose from $495 to $557, with the annual plan nearly doubling from $137 to $253. Play purchases went from $26 to $118. Ads, purchases and Play add up to $1,370, almost unchanged from August. Fewer and fewer people are coming in, but the ones who stay are paying. I don't know how long that will hold.

TravelMemoryMap's purchases were $688, a personal record for the second month running. New downloads fell, yet the 1-year Supporter Plan rose from $286 to $313.

YOU Receipt moved the other way. New downloads rose 60%, from 1,985 to 3,147, but sales fell from August's $603 to $371. The $19 one-time purchase dropped by nearly half, and the weekly plan grew instead. App Store sales per download went from $0.25 to $0.10.

One-time purchases sold in other apps too. HomeKeep, TankMinder and WatchKeep each sold one, all priced in the $16 range. The 7 apps released in August got 181 new downloads combined in September, and their sales were $6.04, from EyeMole and nokki.

Google Play has grown every month since July: $60, $142, then $192. In September UNCHIKUN's monthly plan made $118, so UNCHIKUN is now number one on Play as well.

NextAction

この数字を見て、来月やることを3つ決めました。Based on these numbers, I picked three things to do next month.

  1. うんちくんのTikTokを始める。新規ダウンロードが2か月で半分になったので、ストア検索の外から入ってくる人を動画で増やすStart a TikTok for UNCHIKUN. New downloads halved in two months, so I'll use video to bring in people from outside store search
  2. 旅行思い出マップとあなたレシートでもTikTokの投稿を作って、どのアプリの動画がバズりやすいかを比べるMake TikTok posts for TravelMemoryMap and YOU Receipt too, and compare which app's videos take off more easily
  3. 投稿ごとに計測リンクを付けて、伸びた動画からダウンロードが何件来たかを10月号で出すPut a tracking link on every post, and report in the October issue how many downloads came from the videos that took off

柱の2本は課金が増え、広告が落ちた月でした。10月はTikTokで入口を増やしにいきます。A month where my two pillars grew purchases and lost ad revenue. In October I'm going to open more doors with TikTok.

それでは、また来月の報告で。See you in next month's report.

数値について。AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額で、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。3つとも暦月の1日から末日で区切っています。App Store Connectの財務報告はAppleの会計月で締めるので、同じ月でも対象期間がずれ、画面に出る金額とは一致しません。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とも一致しません。
About the numbers. AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. All three are cut by calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. App Store Connect's financial reports close on Apple's fiscal months, so the period covered shifts even for the same month, and the amounts won't match what's shown there. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts either. Dollar amounts are converted from yen at the ECB month-end rate (¥157.00 per US dollar on 2026-09-30), and every figure in this issue, including last month's, uses that same rate.
