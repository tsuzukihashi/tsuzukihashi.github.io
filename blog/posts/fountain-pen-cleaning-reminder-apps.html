僕が作ったアプリ

万年筆ケア PenKeep

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

吸入方式を選ぶと、洗浄の目安が最初に入ります。カートリッジとコンバーターは4週間ごと、ピストンなどの吸入式は6週間ごと、アイドロッパーは8週間ごとです。目安は2週間から12週間の中で直せます。

通知は洗浄の2週間前と3日前と当日です。過ぎたら、洗ったと記録するまで毎日鳴ります。最後に書いた日からしばらく経ったペンには、乾いてるかもと出るので、書くか洗うかのきっかけになります。

無料だとペンは3本まで登録できます。ペンケースや箱で分けるのと、お手入れの記録はプレミアムです。インクのコレクションを管理する機能はありません。

インク帳

kazuki korenori · App Storeの評価 3件

どのペンに何色が入っているかを、写真つきのカードで一覧にできるアプリです。洗浄の目安を過ぎたペンが先頭に並び、インクを入れたままの日数を通知で知らせてくれます。

インクの管理が細かくて、シェーンやシマー、残量まで残せます。無料だとペン5本とインク10本までで、登録の数のほかは全部の機能が無料です。有料版は買い切りです。

インクノート - 万年筆・インク管理

towa imamoto · App Storeの評価 0件

インクを入れる、洗浄するを記録すると、充填の履歴が残ります。インクを入れたまま使わずにいると、決めた日数で通知が来ます。ホーム画面のウィジェットで、充填中のペンと放置している日数も見られます。

ペン先の調整や修理の履歴を、依頼先や費用つきで残せるのも特徴です。無料だとペン3本とインク5本までです。

洗浄の頻度はどう決めるか

どのくらいで洗うかは、吸入方式やインクの種類、書く頻度で変わります。PenKeepの目安はあくまで出発点です。

ペンやインクの説明書、買ったお店の案内に合わせて決めたら、その日数をアプリに入れておけば忘れずに済みます。

どれを選ぶか

  • インクの色や残りの量まで細かく残したいなら、インク帳
  • ペン先の調整や修理の履歴も残したいなら、インクノート
  • ペンごとの洗浄の日を数えて、過ぎたら毎日知らせてほしいなら、PenKeep

Each app, one by one

I made this

PenKeep: Fountain Pen Care

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Pick the filling system and a cleaning interval is filled in for you. Cartridge and converter pens start at every 4 weeks, piston and other built-in fillers at every 6 weeks, and eyedroppers at every 8 weeks. You can change it to anything from 2 to 12 weeks.

It notifies you 2 weeks before, 3 days before, and on the day. After that, it reminds you every day until you log a cleaning. A pen you haven't written with in a while shows a may-be-drying label, which nudges you to either write with it or clean it.

The free version holds up to 3 pens. Grouping by pen case or box and keeping a care log are Premium features. It doesn't manage an ink collection.

Inkcho

kazuki korenori · 3 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This app shows which ink is in which pen on cards with photos. Pens past their cleaning guideline move to the top, and it notifies you how many days ink has been sitting in a pen.

Its ink tracking is detailed, down to shading, shimmer, and how much is left. The free version holds 5 pens and 10 inks, and every feature except the item limit is free. The paid version is a one-time purchase.

Ink Note: Fountain Pen and Ink Log

towa imamoto · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

When you log filling or cleaning a pen, it builds a fill history. If a pen sits with ink in it, you get a notification after the number of days you set. A Home Screen widget shows inked pens and how long they've been sitting.

It also keeps a history of nib adjustments and repairs, with who did them and what they cost. The free version holds 3 pens and 5 inks.

How to decide how often to flush

How often to flush depends on the filling system, the type of ink, and how often you write. PenKeep's intervals are only a starting point.

Settle on a number based on your pen's and ink's instructions or what the shop recommends, then put it in the app so you don't have to remember.

Which one to pick

  • If you want detailed records of ink colors and how much is left, Inkcho
  • If you also want a history of nib adjustments and repairs, Ink Note
  • If you want each pen's cleaning date counted down, with a reminder every day once it passes, PenKeep

FAQ

Are they free?
All three are free to download. By number of pens, PenKeep and Ink Note are free for up to 3, and Inkcho for up to 5.
A pen I haven't used in a while won't write.
None of these apps fix that for you. For a pen that's dried up, ask the shop where you bought it or the maker.
Do they work on Android?
PenKeep is iPhone only. The App Store descriptions of the other two don't mention an Android version.
