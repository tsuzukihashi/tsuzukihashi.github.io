まずはiPhoneの写真アプリで

アプリを入れなくても、iPhoneの写真アプリで調べられることがあります。花の写真を開いたとき、下の情報ボタンに葉っぱのマークが付いていたら、それをタップします。

植物の名前の候補と、似た写真が出ます。マークが出ない写真もあるので、そのときはアプリの出番ですね。

FlowerLens - 花AI図鑑

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

花を撮るか、写真ライブラリから選ぶと、AIが名前の候補を出します。和名と英名、科、開花時期、花の色、花言葉、誕生花の月までは無料で見られます。

毒がある花には、そのことも表示します。季節ごとに咲く花と、月ごとの誕生花を見られるカレンダーもあります。

無料で調べられるのは2回までです。何回でも調べたいときや、日当たりや水やりなどの育て方を見たいときはプレミアムです。調べるときはネットにつながっている必要があります。

PictureThis：植物識別・育て方園芸図鑑

Glority Global Group Ltd. · App Storeの評価 100,899件

Google Play版

評価が100,899件あって、この中ではいちばん使われています。説明文では40万種以上の植物を識別できると書かれています。

名前を調べるだけでなく、病気の診断、育て方、水やりの通知、日当たりの計測まであります。育てる人のための機能が厚いアプリです。Google PlayにAndroid版もあります。

GreenSnap - 植物・花の名前が判る写真共有アプリ

GREENSNAP, INC. · App Storeの評価 20,663件

Google Play版

植物と花が好きな人が写真を投稿し合うSNSで、評価は20,663件です。名前のわからない植物を撮ると、名前を教えてくれるカメラがあります。

アプリの中の植物図鑑で、育て方や日当たり、水やりの時期も調べられます。Google PlayにAndroid版もあります。

Start with the iPhone Photos app

You may not need an app at all. Open a flower photo in the Photos app, and if the info button at the bottom has a small leaf on it, tap it.

You'll see a suggested plant name along with similar photos. The leaf doesn't show up on every photo, and that's where an app comes in.

The three apps at a glance

The apps differ in what they tell you after the name. The right one depends on whether you want to grow the plant or just know what you saw.

FlowerLens - Flower AI Guide

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Take a photo of a flower or pick one from your library, and the AI suggests what it is. The Japanese and English names, plant family, blooming season, colors, flower meanings, and birth month are all free.

If a flower is toxic, it tells you. There is also a calendar showing which flowers bloom each season and the birth flowers for each month.

You can identify two flowers for free. Unlimited identifications and care tips like sunlight and watering come with Premium. You need an internet connection to identify a flower.

PictureThis - Plant Identifier

Glority Global Group Ltd. · 100,899 App Store ratings

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

With 100,899 ratings, this is by far the most used of the three. Its description says it can identify more than 400,000 plants.

Beyond identifying plants, it diagnoses diseases, gives care plans, reminds you to water, and measures light. It's built for people who grow plants. There's also an Android version on Google Play.

GreenSnap - Plant and Flower Photo Community

GREENSNAP, INC. · 20,663 App Store ratings

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

It's a social app where plant and flower lovers share photos, with 20,663 ratings. It has a camera feature that tells you the name of a plant you don't know.

Its built-in plant encyclopedia covers care, sunlight, and when to water. There is also an Android version on Google Play.

Which one to pick

  • If the name is all you need, try the iPhone Photos app first
  • If you want help caring for plants at home, PictureThis
  • If you'd like to see and share flower photos with others, GreenSnap
  • If you want the flower's meaning and birth month along with its name, FlowerLens

FAQ

Are they free?
All three are free to download. FlowerLens lets you identify two flowers for free, and after that it is Premium. Looking things up in the iPhone Photos app is free.
Which one works on Android?
Of these three, PictureThis and GreenSnap are also on Google Play. FlowerLens is iPhone only.
Can they tell me if a flower is safe for kids or pets?
FlowerLens and PictureThis both flag toxic plants, but treat that as a guide only. If a child or pet has eaten something, contact a doctor or vet right away.
