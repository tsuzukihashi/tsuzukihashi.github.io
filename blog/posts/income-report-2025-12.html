分析Analysis

81,754円。前月の2.1倍で、初めて月8万円台に乗りました。

要因ははっきりしています。旅行思い出マップのダウンロードが903件から3,826件まで伸びました。年末は今年の旅行を振り返りたくなる時期で、このアプリと相性がいい。そして増えたダウンロードの分だけサブスクも素直についてきた。中でも1年間サポータープランが32件売れたのが大きくて、これは1年分の売上を先にもらえる契約です。

一方でAdMobは20,777円と横ばいでした。広告は毎日開かれるかどうかで決まるので、ダウンロードが増えてもツール系アプリではこうなります。

$522. That's 2.1x last month, and the first time I've reached the $511 range.

The reason is clear. TravelMemoryMap downloads went from 903 to 3,826. The end of the year is when people want to look back on the year's trips, and that suits this app well. Subscriptions followed the extra downloads in step. The biggest piece was 32 sales of the 1-year Supporter Plan, a contract that pays a full year's revenue up front.

AdMob, on the other hand, was flat at $133. Ad revenue depends on whether an app gets opened every day, so for a utility app this is what happens even when downloads grow.

NextAction

この数字を見て、来月やることを3つ決めました。Based on these numbers, I picked three things to do next month.

  1. お正月も新規獲得が見込めるので、1月も手を緩めないNew Year's should bring in new users too, so I won't let up in January
  2. サブスクを嫌う層に向けて、英語圏向けアプリで買い切りプランを試すTry one-time purchase plans in my English-market apps, for people who dislike subscriptions
  3. うんちくんの海外展開を準備するPrepare UNCHIKUN for overseas markets

ダウンロードが増えればサブスクは伸びる。次はそれを取り切る仕組みです。More downloads mean more subscriptions. Next is building the system that captures all of them.

数値について。AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額で、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。3つとも暦月の1日から末日で区切っています。App Store Connectの財務報告はAppleの会計月で締めるので、同じ月でも対象期間がずれ、画面に出る金額とは一致しません。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とも一致しません。
About the numbers. AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. All three are cut by calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. App Store Connect's financial reports close on Apple's fiscal months, so the period covered shifts even for the same month, and the amounts won't match what's shown there. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts either. Dollar amounts are converted from yen at the ECB month-end rate (¥156.67 per US dollar on 2025-12-31), and every figure in this issue, including last month's, uses that same rate.
