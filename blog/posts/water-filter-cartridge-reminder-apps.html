フィルター交換 HomeKeep

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

浄水器やエアコンなど、家電の種類を選ぶと交換の周期が最初から入ります。浄水器なら3か月ごとです。周期は1か月から2年までの中で選び直せるので、説明書の日数に合わせて直してください。

通知は7日前と前日と当日です。期限を過ぎたら、交換したと記録するまで毎日鳴ります。交換が近い順に並ぶので、家じゅうのフィルターのうち次にどれを替えるかが一覧で分かります。

無料だとフィルターは3件まで登録できて、通知とウィジェットも使えます。型番や買い直しのリンクを残すのはプレミアムです。iPhone専用です。

浄水器日記：浄水器管理・フィルター交換通知

HAO CHANG KUO · App Storeの評価 0件

名前のとおり浄水器のためのアプリです。フィルターが何段もある浄水器でも、段階ごとに交換の周期を分けて設定できます。据え置き型やアンダーシンク型、ポット型にも使えると書かれています。

プッシュ通知のほかにメールでも知らせてくれて、家族と共有もできます。浄水器を家族で使っていて、誰が替えたか分からなくなる家に向いていますね。

かえどき - 交換時期・掃除リマインダー

Hiroyuki Kawada · App Storeの評価 2件

エアコンのフィルター、浄水器のカートリッジ、換気扇のフィルターなど、よくあるものは交換の目安が最初から入っています。期限の日の好きな時刻に通知が来て、やったと押せば次の日付が決まります。

型番を登録しておくと、Amazonや楽天でそのまま探せます。保証書を写真で残せるのも、ほかの2本には無いところです。

アプリを入れたくないなら

iPhoneに最初から入っているリマインダーでもできます。交換した日から数えた日付でリマインダーを作って、繰り返しをカスタムで3か月ごとのように設定します。

ただ、交換が遅れた月は次の日付を自分で直すことになります。交換した日から数え直してくれるのが、専用のアプリのいいところです。

どれを選ぶか

  • フィルターのほかに歯ブラシや保証書もまとめたいなら、かえどき
  • 家電のフィルターを交換が近い順に並べて、過ぎたら毎日知らせてほしいなら、HomeKeep

Your cartridge's manual sets the date

Every water filter cartridge has its own replacement guideline. Whether it's every few months or every so many liters, it's printed in the manual or on the cartridge box.

The hard part is remembering the date. From here on, I compare three apps that count from the day you swapped the cartridge and remind you.

The three apps at a glance

The main difference is whether an app focuses on water purifiers or keeps all the filters in your home in one list.

I made this

HomeKeep - Filter Reminder

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Pick the type of appliance, such as a water purifier or an air conditioner, and a replacement cycle is filled in for you. For a water purifier, it starts at every 3 months. You can change it to anything from 1 month to 2 years, so set it to match your manual.

It notifies you 7 days before, the day before, and on the day. Once the date passes, it keeps notifying you every day until you log the swap. Filters are listed by which one is due next, so you can see at a glance what to replace first.

The free version holds up to 3 filters, with notifications and widgets included. Saving model numbers and links to buy the same cartridge again is a Premium feature. It's iPhone only.

Water Purifier Diary

HAO CHANG KUO · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

As the name says, it's built for water purifiers. Even if yours has several filter stages, you can set a separate cycle for each one. The description says it works for countertop, under-sink, and pitcher types.

Besides push notifications, it can also email you, and you can share it with your family. It suits a household where everyone uses the purifier and nobody remembers who changed it last.

Kaedoki: Replacement and Cleaning Reminder

Hiroyuki Kawada · 2 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Common items like air conditioner filters, water filter cartridges, and range hood filters come with a replacement guideline already set. You get a notification on the due date at a time you pick, and tapping done sets the next date.

If you save the model number, you can search for it on Amazon or Rakuten straight away. Keeping photos of warranty cards is something the other two don't offer.

If you'd rather not install an app

You can use the Reminders app that comes with your iPhone. Create a reminder on the next replacement date, then set a custom repeat, for example every 3 months.

The catch is that if you replace it late, you have to fix the next date yourself. Counting again from the day you actually swapped it is what the dedicated apps do for you.

Which one to pick

  • If you want to track only your water purifier, stage by stage, and share it with family, Water Purifier Diary
  • If you want toothbrushes and warranties in the same place as your filters, Kaedoki
  • If you want your appliance filters sorted by what's due next, with a reminder every day once one is overdue, HomeKeep

FAQ

Where do I find the replacement timing?
It's in the cartridge's manual or on the box. If you can't find it, look up the model number on the purifier maker's website.
Are they free?
All three are free to download. HomeKeep's free version holds up to 3 filters, and adding more is a paid feature.
Can I track my air conditioner filter too?
HomeKeep and Kaedoki can. Water Purifier Diary is made for water purifiers.

HomeKeep doesn't have a single rating yet, so I'd be glad to hear from the first person who tries it.
