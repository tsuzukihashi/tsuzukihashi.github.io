僕が作ったアプリ

ZennChat: 禅の日々

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

Google Play版

仏教伝道協会の仏教聖典をもとに、毎日1つの一節と、その日にできる小さな実践を届けます。ホームの今日の道のりで、読む、行う、坐る、書く、学ぶを順にたどれます。

坐禅タイマーは5分から30分で、音声の案内は付けても付けなくても選べます。教え365件と、禅語や作法などの用語370件を引けて、別れがつらいときのように、心の状態から言葉を探すこともできます。

仏教の教えに根ざしたAIに悩みを相談できて、無料では通算3回までです。プレミアムにすると相談が無制限になり、修行プランが9本と配色のテーマが増えます。日本語と英語に対応していて、Android版もあります。

般若心経365〜お経入門

ZEN APP · App Storeの評価 2,689件

評価が2,689件あって、この中ではいちばん使われています。仏教を誰にでもわかりやすく、をテーマにしたアプリで、ゴーン、ポクポクという音に合わせて般若心経を自分で唱えて覚えます。

大きな文字で読めて、作法はなるべく宗派によらない形にしてあります。伝統仏教の僧侶が監修していて、集められる般若カードもあります。

AI Hotoke: AI 仏陀, ブッダ

BINIX LTD · App Storeの評価 261件

仏教の教えや東洋の智慧を取り入れたAIに、いまの悩みを入力して問いかけるアプリです。

年額と週額のサブスクリプションがあって、期間中は相談を回数の制限なく使えます。無料でどこまで使えるかは、説明文に書かれていませんでした。

ぶっかつ - 仏教で心を整える仏活アプリ

Hirade Keishi · App Storeの評価 7件

仏教をもとにした心の実践アプリで、3分から始められます。今日の言葉、呼吸と瞑想、お経を読む機能が無料です。

相談のチャットは、最初の3日間は回数の制限がなく、4日目からは無料だと1日2回までです。サブスクリプションで回数の制限と広告がなくなります。

アプリを入れたくないなら

仏教聖典は本でも読めます。ZennChatの一節も、この本の文章を許可をもらって使っています。

坐禅の作法を直接教わりたいなら、お寺の坐禅会に行くのがいちばん確かです。アプリは、家で毎日少しずつ続けるためのものですね。

よくある質問

宗派は関係ありますか？
般若心経365は、作法をなるべく宗派によらない形にしていると説明文にあります。ZennChatの一節は、仏教伝道協会の仏教聖典からとっています。
AIの相談に、つらい気持ちを話してもいいですか？
AIの相談は、教えをもとにした話し相手です。つらい気持ちが続くときは、身近な人や専門の相談窓口に話してください。
英語でも使えますか？

ZennChat: Daily Practice

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

Based on The Teaching of Buddha from the Society for the Promotion of Buddhism, it brings you one passage a day plus a small practice for that day. The home screen's path for today takes you through reading, doing, sitting, writing, and learning in order.

The meditation timer runs from 5 to 30 minutes, with optional voice guidance. You can look up 365 teachings and 370 terms like Zen phrases and etiquette, and also find words by how you feel, such as when a parting is hard.

You can talk through what is on your mind with an AI grounded in Buddhist teaching, up to three times in total for free. Premium makes it unlimited and adds nine practice programs and color themes. It works in Japanese and English, and there is an Android version.

HeartSutra 365: Buddhism chant

ZEN APP · 2,689 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

With 2,689 ratings, this is the most used of the four. Its theme is making Buddhism easy for anyone, and you learn the Heart Sutra by chanting it yourself along with the bell and wooden drum sounds.

The text is large and easy to read, and the etiquette is kept as non-sectarian as possible. It is supervised by a priest of traditional Buddhism, and there are Heart Sutra cards to collect.

AI Buddha: AI Hotoke

BINIX LTD · 261 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This app lets you type in what is troubling you and put the question to an AI that draws on Buddhist teaching and Eastern wisdom.

There are yearly and weekly subscriptions, and while subscribed you can ask as much as you like. The description doesn't say how much you can do for free.

Zen Chat: Buddhist Meditation

Hirade Keishi · 7 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

A Buddhist practice app you can start in three minutes. Words for today, breathing and meditation, and sutra reading are free.

For the first three days, the chat has no limit, and from day four the free plan allows two a day. A subscription removes the limit and the ads.

If you'd rather not install an app

The Teaching of Buddha is also available as a book. ZennChat's daily passages come from it, used with permission.

If you want to learn meditation etiquette in person, going to a sitting at a temple is the surest way. Apps are for keeping it up a little each day at home.

Which one to pick

  • If you want to learn to chant the Heart Sutra, HeartSutra 365
  • If talking with an AI is the main thing and you want to ask often, AI Hotoke
  • If you want three-minute meditation and daily words for free, Bukkatsu
  • If you want a daily passage, meditation, and a journal in one app, or need Android, ZennChat

FAQ

Does it matter which school of Buddhism I follow?
HeartSutra 365 says its etiquette is kept as non-sectarian as possible. ZennChat's passages come from The Teaching of Buddha by the Society for the Promotion of Buddhism.
Is it okay to tell the AI about painful feelings?
The AI is a conversation partner grounded in the teachings. If painful feelings keep going, please talk to someone close to you or a counseling service.
Can I use them in English?
ZennChat supports Japanese and English. I didn't check English support for the other three for this article.

ZennChat doesn't have any ratings yet, so I'd be happy if your thoughts on today's passage were the first one 🙏
