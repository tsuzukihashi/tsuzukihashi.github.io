僕が作ったアプリ

Today -今日は令和何年で何の日？-

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 3件

開くと、今日が令和何年か、何曜日か、六曜、祝日かどうかが並び、その下に今日の記念日や年中行事が出ます。次の祝日まであと何日か、今日の月の形、誕生花と花言葉も1画面にまとめています。

ウィジェットは、和暦、今日は何の日、祝日のカウントダウン、月の満ち欠けの4種類です。和暦と今日は何の日はロック画面にも置けるので、書類に令和何年と書くときにロック画面を見るだけで済みます。

見られるのは今日の情報だけで、ほかの日を調べる機能はありません。全部無料で、アプリの中に広告が出ます。

今日は何の日?

kazuhiro aonuma · App Storeの評価 4,107件

評価が4,107件あって、4本の中でいちばん使われています。開くとすぐ、今日の記念日、歴史の出来事、誕生日の有名人が一覧で出ます。

日付を送れば365日どの日でも調べられて、自分の誕生日や記念日も登録できます。決めた時刻に今日の記念日を通知する機能や、Siriへの対応もあります。広告は買い切りで消せます。

今日は何の日ウィジェット

Satoko Kondo · App Storeの評価 3件

今日は何の日かが分かるアプリで、ホーム画面やロック画面にウィジェットとして置けます。

説明文はこの2点だけで、ほかに何が出るのかは書かれていませんでした。

作者が9割知らなかった【今日の雑学366】今日は何の日アプリ

Kazuyuki Suda · App Storeの評価 3件

1日に1つ、366日分の記念日と、それにちなんだ雑学をイラストと音楽と一緒に見せるアプリです。説明文に、収録している記念日の一覧が全部載っています。

記念日の名前だけでなく、話のタネになる雑学まで欲しい人に合います。ウィジェットや料金については、説明文に書かれていませんでした。

アプリを入れたくないなら

今日が令和何年かだけなら、iPhoneの設定で暦法を和暦にする手もあります。ただ、カレンダーなどの年の表示が全部和暦になります。

記念日は、検索で「今日は何の日」と打てば出てきます。毎日ウィジェットで目に入れたいなら、アプリのほうが手間がかかりません。

Each app, one by one

I made this

Today - Japanese Era Year & On This Day

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 3 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Open it and you see the Reiwa year, the day of the week, the rokuyo, and whether it's a holiday, with today's anniversaries and seasonal events below. The days until the next holiday, tonight's moon phase, and the flower of the day with its meaning are on the same screen.

There are four widgets: the era year, today's anniversaries, a holiday countdown, and the moon phase. The era year and anniversaries widgets also go on the Lock Screen, so when you need to write the Reiwa year on a form, a glance at your Lock Screen is enough.

It only shows today. There's no way to look up other dates. Everything is free, with ads in the app.

What Day Is It?

kazuhiro aonuma · 4,107 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

With 4,107 ratings, it's the most used of the four. The moment you open it, you get a list of today's anniversaries, historical events, and famous people born today.

Step through the dates to look up any day of the year, and add your own birthdays and anniversaries. It can also notify you of today's anniversary at a time you choose, and it works with Siri. Ads can be removed with a one-time purchase.

On This Day Widget

Satoko Kondo · 3 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

It tells you what today is known for, and you can put it on your Home Screen or Lock Screen as a widget.

That's all the description says, so it isn't clear what else it shows.

Daily Trivia 366: What Day Is It

Kazuyuki Suda · 3 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Once a day, it shows one of 366 anniversaries with a bit of related trivia, along with an illustration and music. The description lists every anniversary it covers.

It suits people who want trivia to talk about, not just the name of the day. The description doesn't mention widgets or pricing.

If you'd rather not install an app

If all you need is the Reiwa year, you can switch your iPhone's calendar setting to the Japanese calendar. But then years across the system, like in the Calendar app, all show in the Japanese format.

For anniversaries, searching for what day it is will turn them up. If you want to see them every day on a widget, an app saves you the effort.

Which one to pick

  • If you want anniversaries, events, and famous birthdays together, and to look up other dates, What Day Is It?
  • If you want trivia that goes with each anniversary, Daily Trivia 366
  • If you want the Reiwa year, rokuyo, holidays, and the moon on widgets along with anniversaries, Today

FAQ

Can I show the Reiwa year on my Lock Screen?
Yes, with Today's era year widget you can show today's date in the Japanese calendar on your Lock Screen.
Do they show auspicious days like taian and butsumetsu?
Today and What Day Is It? both show the rokuyo, so you can check it when picking a date for a wedding or similar.
Are they free?
All four are free to download. Today and What Day Is It? both show ads, and What Day Is It? lets you remove them with a one-time purchase.
