分析Analysis

338,294円。前月から6%減りましたが、月30万円台は守りました。

先月の宣言どおり、うんちくんにAI機能を載せてUNCHIKUN: Poop Log & AI Healthに進化させました。名前にAIを入れたことでストアでの見え方が変わり、ダウンロードは33,652件。単体で月17.8万円を稼ぎ、柱の座を固めています。

もうひとつのトピックはGoogle Playの月1万円超えです。1月に102円だったのが、5ヶ月で100倍になりました。特別年額のオファーが14件売れていて、Androidユーザーにも課金意欲はちゃんとあります。

気がかりはうんちくんのeCPMが143円まで下がったことです。2月は504円でした。海外比率が上がった影響なので成長の証ではあるものの、広告フォーマットは見直す余地があります。

$2,124. Down 6% from last month, but it held the $1,884 range.

As I said last month, I added AI features to UNCHIKUN and turned it into UNCHIKUN: Poop Log & AI Health. Putting AI in the name changed how it shows up in the store, and downloads reached 33,652. On its own it earned $1,118 this month, cementing its place as the main pillar.

The other topic is Google Play crossing $63 a month. It was $0.64 in January, so it has grown 100x in five months. The special annual offer sold 14 times. Android users are clearly willing to pay.

The worry is that UNCHIKUN's eCPM dropped to $0.90. It was $3.16 in February. That comes from the overseas share going up, so it's a sign of growth, but there's room to rethink the ad formats.

NextAction

この数字を見て、来月やることを3つ決めました。Based on these numbers, I picked three things to do next month.

  1. リワード広告のような高単価フォーマットの比率を上げるRaise the share of higher-paying formats such as rewarded ads

派手さはないけど、こういう月が下限を作ってくれる。Nothing flashy, but months like this are what build the floor.

数値について。AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額で、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。3つとも暦月の1日から末日で区切っています。App Store Connectの財務報告はAppleの会計月で締めるので、同じ月でも対象期間がずれ、画面に出る金額とは一致しません。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とも一致しません。
About the numbers. AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. All three are cut by calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. App Store Connect's financial reports close on Apple's fiscal months, so the period covered shifts even for the same month, and the amounts won't match what's shown there. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts either. Dollar amounts are converted from yen at the ECB month-end rate (¥159.27 per US dollar on 2026-05-29), and every figure in this issue, including last month's, uses that same rate.
