僕が作ったアプリ

Kimochi かわいい気分日記

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

白いおもちの妖精モチと一緒につける気分日記です。5段階の気分をタップすると、モチが同じ表情と色に変わります。仕事、家族、睡眠のようなタグとひとことは、書きたいときだけで大丈夫です。

記録は無料で何回でもできて、ホームには最近の7件と、続いた日数が出ます。毎日決めた時刻に通知で声をかけてもらうこともできます。

正直に書くと、気分の波を見るカレンダーと、時間帯、曜日、タグ別の分析はProです。モチの着せ替えは無料だとリボンだけで、はっぱ、かんむり、おほしさまとCSVの書き出しもProです。記録は端末の中だけに保存されます。

デイリオ日記：気分・習慣・毎日を簡単に記録できる日記帳

Relaxio s.r.o. · App Storeの評価 3,045件

評価が3,045件あって、この中ではいちばん使われています。気分を選んで、その日にした活動を足すだけで日記になります。

強いのはグラフです。週、月、年のグラフや、1年を色で並べる表示で、どの活動の日に気分が上がるかを見られます。目標や習慣の記録にも使えて、PINや生体認証でロックもできます。

波きぶん

Masahiro Akebi · App Storeの評価 8件

1日に何回でも記録できるのが特徴です。朝はすっきり、昼はもやもや、のような1日の中の波まで残せます。波のキャラクターのなみちゃんが、気分に合わせて変わります。

記録、カレンダー、基本の統計は完全無料と書かれています。無料のまま波をグラフで見たいなら、この4本の中ではいちばん向いています。

気分の波 - ウィジェット気分記録グラフ

Shuhei Yamamoto · App Storeの評価 0件

アプリを開かずに、ホーム画面のウィジェットから1タップで記録します。iOS 18ならコントロールセンターやアクションボタンからも記録できます。

無料で見られるのは直近7日のグラフです。行動と気分の関係や、時間帯と曜日の傾向は、買い切りのProです。

アプリを入れたくないなら

手帳にムードトラッカーを書く方法もあります。1日1マス、その日の気分の色で塗るだけです。

iPhoneのヘルスケアアプリにも、気分を記録する機能があります。最初から入っているので、まずはこちらで続くか試すのも手ですね。

Each app, one by one

I made this

Kimochi - Cute Mood Diary

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

It's a mood diary you keep with Mochi, a little white rice-cake fairy. Tap one of 5 moods, and Mochi changes to the same face and color. Tags like work, family, or sleep, and a short note, are only there if you want them.

Logging is free with no limit, and the home screen shows your last 7 entries and how many days in a row you've logged. You can also get a reminder at the same time each day.

To be upfront, the calendar for seeing your ups and downs, and the breakdown by time of day, weekday, and tag, are Pro. The only free outfit for Mochi is the bow. The leaf, crown, and star, plus CSV export, are Pro too. Everything you log stays on your phone.

Daylio Journal - Mood Tracker

Relaxio s.r.o. · 3,045 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

With 3,045 ratings, it's the most used of the four. Pick a mood, add what you did that day, and that's your entry.

Its strength is the charts. Weekly, monthly, and yearly graphs, plus a view of the whole year in colored squares, show which activities go with your better days. It also tracks goals and habits, and locks with a PIN or Face ID.

Mood Wave - Daily Mood Diary

Masahiro Akebi · 8 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

You can log as many times a day as you like, so it catches the ups and downs within a single day, like clear-headed in the morning and foggy by noon. A wave character called Nami-chan changes with your mood.

The description says logging, the calendar, and basic stats are completely free. If you want to see your pattern as a graph without paying, it's the best fit of the four.

Mood Waves: Widget Mood Log and Graph

Shuhei Yamamoto · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

You log with one tap from a Home Screen widget without opening the app. On iOS 18, you can also log from Control Center or the Action button.

For free you get a 7-day graph. How your activities relate to your mood and your time-of-day and weekday patterns are part of a one-time Pro purchase.

If you'd rather not install an app

You can also keep a mood tracker in a paper planner. One square per day, colored to match how you felt.

The Health app on your iPhone also has a feature for logging your state of mind. It's already there, so it's a fine way to see whether the habit sticks.

Which one to pick

  • If you want to see the pattern for free and log several times a day, Mood Wave
  • If you want to log activities and habits too and look back over a whole year, Daylio
  • If opening an app is what made you quit before, Mood Waves
  • If you want to start by keeping it light with a cute character, Kimochi

FAQ

Is an app enough if my low moods keep going?
All four are for logging and looking back. None of them diagnose or treat anything. If you've been feeling low for a while, please talk to a doctor or another professional.
Are they free?
All four are free to download and to log in. The difference is looking back. Mood Wave includes the calendar and basic stats for free, while Kimochi's calendar and analysis are Pro.
I'm worried someone might see my entries
Kimochi, Mood Wave, and Mood Waves all say entries stay on your phone. If you want a lock, Daylio and Mood Wave offer a PIN or passcode.
