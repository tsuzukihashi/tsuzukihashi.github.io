僕が作ったアプリ

Monivo 声で起こし合うアラーム

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 1件

Google Play版

恋人や友だちが録った15秒の声が、そのまま目覚ましやリマインダーになります。届いた声のどれで起きるかは、自分で選べます。明日は遅刻できないから激しめに起こして、のように、ほしい声をフレンドにお願いすることもできます。

フレンドがいなくても、世界のユーザーが録った声が時間帯やタグに合わせて届くので、毎朝ちがう声で起きられます。自分だけにのこす声として録音して、自分の声で起きることもできます。

録音、送受信、フレンドの追加、世界の声のアラームは無料です。プレミアムは月額か年額で、声の保存が300件まで、録音が30秒まで伸びて、広告が消えます。Google PlayにAndroid版もあります。

Oshiton - 推し・子供の声で目覚ましアラーム

AMAIRU LLC · App Storeの評価 8件

好きな声を自分で録音して、アラームやタイマーの音にするアプリです。子どもの声やパートナーの応援、推しのイベントで録った声などを想定しています。

30秒まで高音質で録れて、スマホに保存した動画から声を切り出すこともできます。平日用と休日用のように、アラームごとに声を変えられて、録った声をiPhoneの着信音として書き出すこともできます。iOS 26.1以降が必要です。

こけこ - 友達の声で起きる目覚まし

Stellaps Inc. · App Storeの評価 0件

夜9時に友だちへ、明日起こしてと通知が届き、友だちが30秒の声を吹き込むと、それが翌朝のアラームになる作りです。起きたことは友だちに伝わって、声で返事ができます。

友だちが録り忘れても、前に届いた声か自分の声、それも無ければ標準の音に切り替わるので、鳴らない朝が無いようにしてあります。目覚ましの機能は全部無料で、声の主を選ぶのと、受け取った声を10本より多く取っておくのは月額か年額のProです。

アプリを入れたくないなら

iPhoneの時計アプリのアラーム音には、着信音を選べます。録音した声をGarageBandで着信音として書き出せば、それをアラーム音にできます。

ただ、手順が多くて、声を変えるたびにやり直しになります。相手から声が届くのを待つだけで済むのは、アプリのいいところですね。

Monivo: Wake Up by Voice

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 1 App Store rating

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

A 15-second voice recorded by your partner or a friend becomes your alarm or reminder. You choose which of the voices you've received will wake you. You can even ask a friend for a specific kind of voice, like asking them to be extra loud because you can't be late tomorrow.

Even without friends on the app, voices recorded by users around the world arrive matched to the time of day and tags, so you can wake up to a different voice every morning. You can also record a voice just for yourself and wake up to your own.

Recording, sending and receiving, adding friends, and alarms with voices from around the world are free. Premium is monthly or yearly, raises saved voices to 300 and recordings to 30 seconds, and removes the ads. There's also an Android version on Google Play.

Oshiton - Voice Recorder Alarm

AMAIRU LLC · 8 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This app lets you record any voice you love and use it as an alarm or timer sound. It has in mind things like your kid's voice, a pep talk from your partner, or a voice you recorded at your favorite idol's event.

It records up to 30 seconds in high quality, and you can also clip a voice out of a video saved on your phone. You can use a different voice for each alarm, like weekdays and weekends, and export a recording as an iPhone ringtone. It needs iOS 26.1 or later.

Kokeko - Friend's Voice Alarm

Stellaps Inc. · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

At 9 p.m. your friend gets a notification asking them to wake you tomorrow, and the 30-second voice they record becomes your alarm the next morning. When you get up, your friend is told, and you can reply with a voice message.

If your friend forgets to record, it falls back to an earlier voice from them, then to your own voice, then to a standard alarm sound, so there's never a morning with no alarm. All the alarm features are free, and choosing whose voice you get and keeping more than 10 received voices come with Pro, monthly or yearly.

If you'd rather not install an app

In the iPhone Clock app you can pick a ringtone as your alarm sound. If you export a recorded voice as a ringtone with GarageBand, you can use it as your alarm.

The catch is that it takes quite a few steps, and you have to redo them every time you want a new voice. With an app, you just wait for the voice to arrive.

Which one to pick

  • If you want to record your kid's or your favorite idol's voice yourself and use different ones for different alarms, Oshiton
  • If you want a friend to record a voice the night before and reply once you are up, Kokeko
  • If you want voices from people around the world as well as friends, or want it on Android too, Monivo

FAQ

Do they ring in silent mode?
All three say in their descriptions that they ring even in silent mode. All of them need an iPhone on iOS 26 or later.
Can I use them without friends on the app?
Yes. All three let you wake up to your own voice, and Monivo can also wake you with voices from users around the world.
Is any of them on Android?
Of these three, Monivo is the one I could confirm is on Google Play.
