Simple Reminder is a straightforward reminder app designed to help you never forget important tasks.

Key Features

  • Create, edit, and delete reminders
  • Get notified at your specified date and time
  • Repeating reminders (daily, weekly, monthly) - Premium feature
  • Clean and intuitive interface

Perfect For

  • People who tend to forget appointments
  • Those looking for a simple reminder app
  • Anyone who just wants notifications without complex features

Free Version

  • Create up to 3 reminders
  • Basic notification functionality

Premium Version

  • Unlimited reminders
  • Repeating reminders (daily, weekly, monthly)
  • Ad-free experience

Manage your important tasks with Simple Reminder.
