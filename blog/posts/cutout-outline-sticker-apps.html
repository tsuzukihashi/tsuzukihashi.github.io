白いふちならiPhoneだけでできる

iPhoneの写真アプリで、人や物を長押しするとその部分だけを切り抜けます。そこからステッカーを追加して、効果を選ぶと白いふちが付きます。

ただ、ふちの色や太さは自分で決められません。推しの色で縁取りたい、太くしたいとなったら、アプリの出番です。

3本をざっくり比べる

3本とも、切り抜きはスマホの中で済んで、写真を外に送りません。違うのは、縁でどこまで遊べるかと、ほかに何ができるかです。

僕が作ったアプリ

フチドリ！ ~背景透過と縁取り~

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 16件

写真を選ぶと、スマホの中で背景を自動で切り抜きます。縁の色は用意した色から選ぶほかに、好きな色を作ったり、写真の中からスポイトで拾ったりできます。

太さは0から50ptまで、1pt刻みで変えられます。保存すると背景が透明なPNGになるので、ステッカーや別の画像にそのまま使えます。通信しないので、オフラインでも動きます。

無料だと最初に3回保存できて、そのあとは広告を見ると保存できます。買い切りの購入で広告が出なくなります。

背景革命 - 写真・動画の背景透過・モザイク・ぼかし加工

Osawa Shunsuke · App Storeの評価 128件

背景の編集が中心のアプリで、評価は128件です。白い縁を付けてステッカー風にでき、縁の幅や影の向きも細かく決められます。

写真に何人も写っていても、残したい人をタップで選べます。背景をぼかす、差し替える、何枚もまとめて加工する、動画の背景を変えることもできます。Pro版は一度買えばずっと使える買い切りです。

Photo Outline Maker: Editor

Jabed Hasan · App Storeの評価 0件

縁の種類が132もあるのが特徴です。白い線のほかに、点線、光る縁、星やハートの縁があって、幅と色も変えられます。

切り抜いたあとに、背景の色や写真、文字、デコを足して仕上げられます。処理は全部iPhoneの中です。月額と年額のサブスクリプションがあります。説明文は英語だけでした。

A white edge needs nothing but your iPhone

In the iPhone Photos app, press and hold on a person or object to lift it out of the photo. Add it as a sticker, then pick an effect to give it a white edge.

What you can't do is choose the outline color or width. If you want it in a favorite color, or thicker, that's where an app helps.

The three apps at a glance

All three cut out the subject on your phone without uploading your photo. The differences are how far you can play with the outline, and what else they do.

Fuchidori! - Outline Stickers

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 16 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Pick a photo and it automatically removes the background on your phone. For the outline color, choose from presets, make any color you like, or pick one straight from the photo with an eyedropper.

Width goes from 0 to 50 pt in 1 pt steps. It saves as a PNG with a transparent background, ready to use as a sticker or drop into another image. It doesn't need the internet, so it works offline too.

The free version gives you three saves to start, and after that you can save by watching an ad. A one-time purchase removes the ads.

Photo/Video Background Remover

Osawa Shunsuke · 128 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This app is mainly about editing backgrounds, and it has 128 ratings. It can add a white edge for a sticker look, and you can fine-tune the edge width and shadow direction.

If several people are in a photo, you can tap the one you want to keep. It can also blur or replace backgrounds, process several photos at once, and edit video backgrounds. The Pro version is a one-time purchase.

Photo Outline Maker: Editor

Jabed Hasan · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Its standout is 132 outline styles. Besides a plain white line there are dashed lines, glows, stars, and hearts, all with adjustable width and color.

After the cutout, you can add backgrounds, text, and decals to finish it. Everything is processed on the iPhone. It offers monthly and yearly subscriptions. The description is in English only.

Which one to pick

  • If a white edge is enough, start with the iPhone Photos app
  • If you also want to blur or replace backgrounds, or edit video, Background Remover
  • If you want fun outline styles like stars and hearts, Photo Outline Maker
  • If you want an outline in any color and width, saved as a transparent PNG, Fuchidori!

FAQ

Are they free?
All three are free to download. Fuchidori! gives you three free saves, and after that you can save by watching an ad. Background Remover also lets you edit photos in its free version.
Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?
All three say in their descriptions that the cutout is processed on your phone.
Can I save with a transparent background?
All three can. Fuchidori! saves straight to a PNG with a transparent background.
