僕が作ったアプリ

コンタクト交換リマインダー LensLog

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 1件

開封日を入れると交換日を数えて、3日前と前日と当日に知らせます。期限を過ぎたら、交換したと記録するまで毎日鳴ります。目のことなので、ちょっとしつこくしてあります。

買い置きの箱の数を入れておけば、残りが少なくなったときにも知らせます。つけ心地を記録すると、何日目から快適さが落ちるかがグラフで見えます。

無料だとレンズは1種類までで、期限の通知と買い置きは無料で使えます。左右で別の交換日を管理するのはプレミアムです。iPhone専用で、Android版はありません。

コンタクトレンズ交換リマインダー

kazuhiro aonuma · App Storeの評価 4,085件

評価が4,085件もあって、この中ではいちばん使われています。強いのはウィジェットです。ホーム画面やロック画面に残り日数が出て、レンズを替えたらウィジェットのボタンで開始日を直せます。

通知は前日か最終日に1回です。ワンデー、2week、マンスリーを何本でも登録できて、在庫や度数、処方箋の期限もメモできます。基本の機能は全部無料で、課金すると広告が消えます。

コンタクト交換リマインダー - Lens Manager

Yuichi Ishibashi · App Storeの評価 44件

Google Play版

左右で別の交換日を無料で管理できます。1week、2week、1monthに対応していて、Google PlayにAndroid版もあります。

3日前からの通知と、期限を過ぎてからの毎日の通知は、プレミアムの交換忘れ防止モードに入っています。

コンタクト交換管理

Yuto Matsuzaki · App Storeの評価 19件

右目と左目を分けて記録できて、2種類のレンズを併用していてもそれぞれの交換日を管理できます。ワンデーは使った枚数から在庫を数えてくれます。

一部の機能は月額か年額のサブスクリプションです。どの機能が有料なのかは、説明文には書かれていませんでした。

アプリを入れたくないなら

iPhoneに最初から入っているリマインダーでもできます。交換する日にリマインダーを作って、繰り返しをカスタムで2週間ごとにするだけです。

ただ、交換が1日ずれたときは、次の日付を自分で直すことになります。開封日から数え直してくれるのが、専用のアプリのいいところですね。

どれを選ぶか

  • ホーム画面で残り日数が見えれば十分なら、コンタクトレンズ交換リマインダー
  • 左右別を無料で使いたい人や、Androidも使う人は、Lens Manager
  • 期限を過ぎたら毎日鳴らしてほしい、買い置きも一緒に見たいなら、LensLog

Short answer

The quickest fix is an app that counts from the day you open a lens and notifies you before it's due. The most widely used one is Contact Lens Reminder - Widget, with 4,085 ratings. If your left and right lenses start on different days, or you want a reminder every day until you actually swap them, take a look at LensLog, which I made.

LensLog: Contact Lens Reminder

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 1 App Store rating

View on the App Store

Enter the day you opened a lens and it counts to the replacement date, then notifies you 3 days before, the day before, and on the day. Once the date passes, it keeps notifying you every day until you log the swap. It's your eyes, so I made it a little pushy on purpose.

If you enter how many spare boxes you have, it also tells you when you're running low. Log how comfortable each day feels, and a graph shows the day your lenses start to feel worse.

The free version handles one lens type, and the due-date notifications and spare stock are free. Tracking separate dates for each eye is a Premium feature. It's iPhone only, with no Android version.

Contact Lens Reminder - Widget

kazuhiro aonuma · 4,085 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

With 4,085 ratings, this is by far the most used of the four. Its strength is widgets. The days left show up on your Home Screen and Lock Screen, and when you swap lenses you can reset the start date with a button right on the widget.

It notifies you once, either the day before or on the last day. You can register any number of daily, two-week, and monthly lenses, and keep notes on stock, prescription, and when your prescription expires. Every core feature is free, and a purchase removes the ads.

Lens Manager

Yuichi Ishibashi · 44 App Store ratings

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

You can track separate dates for each eye for free. It supports one-week, two-week, and one-month lenses, and there's also an Android version on Google Play.

Notifications from 3 days before, and daily notifications after the date has passed, are part of its Premium mode for people who keep forgetting.

LensCycle - Contact Reminder

Yuto Matsuzaki · 19 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

You can record the right and left eye separately, and keep separate schedules if you use two kinds of lenses. For daily lenses, it counts down your stock from the number you've used.

Some features need a monthly or yearly subscription. The description doesn't say which ones.

If you'd rather not install an app

The Reminders app that comes with your iPhone can do it. Create a reminder on your replacement day and set it to repeat every 2 weeks with a custom repeat.

The catch is that if you swap a day late, you have to fix the next date yourself. Counting again from the day you actually opened the lens is what the dedicated apps are good at.

Which one to pick

  • If seeing the days left on your Home Screen is enough, Contact Lens Reminder - Widget
  • If you want separate left and right dates for free, or you also use Android, Lens Manager
  • If you want a reminder every day after the date until you swap, plus your spare stock in one place, LensLog

FAQ

Are they free?
All four are free to download. Removing ads or unlocking extra features is what costs money.
Which one works on Android?
Of these four, Lens Manager is also on Google Play. LensLog is iPhone only.
Is there any point if I wear daily lenses?
You don't need a replacement date, so it's for keeping an eye on your spare stock. LensLog, Contact Lens Reminder - Widget, and LensCycle all track stock.
Can I keep wearing lenses past the replacement date?
Every one of these apps only shows a guideline date. Whether it's okay to keep wearing them is a question for your eye doctor.
