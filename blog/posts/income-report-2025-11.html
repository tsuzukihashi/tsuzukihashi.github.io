Hi, I'm tsuzuki817 (@tsuzuki817).

This month I'm starting a new series called The Struggling Indie Dev's Road to ¥10M a Month. Every month I'll publish all of my revenue from AdMob, the App Store and Google Play, look at the numbers, and decide what to do next. I plan to keep going until I make ¥10 million a month.

I started indie development in 2016. In ten years I've shipped more than 50 apps, and my first revenue was 21 yen (about $0.19) in July 2019, my third year. I wrote about the road up to that point in the prehistory issue.

The number for the first issue: $244 for the month. That's after putting more than 50 apps on the App Store. This table is exactly why I call myself an indie developer at his limit.

In the breakdown, TravelMemoryMap accounts for 68% of revenue. Its ad eCPM of $4.66 isn't bad. The real problem is that only a few of my apps have any way to make money built in at all. And 1,253 downloads a month just isn't enough volume.

Flip that around and there's room to grow everywhere. From here on, I'll face the numbers every month and make my next move.

数値について。AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額で、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。3つとも暦月の1日から末日で区切っています。App Store Connectの財務報告はAppleの会計月で締めるので、同じ月でも対象期間がずれ、画面に出る金額とは一致しません。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とも一致しません。
About the numbers. AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. All three are cut by calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. App Store Connect's financial reports close on Apple's fiscal months, so the period covered shifts even for the same month, and the amounts won't match what's shown there. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts either. Dollar amounts are converted from yen at the ECB month-end rate (¥156.12 per US dollar on 2025-11-28), and every figure in this issue, including last month's, uses that same rate.
