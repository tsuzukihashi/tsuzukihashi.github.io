What is Jelly Same Puzzle?

The excitement of "Petit Same Puzzle," beloved on HanGame, is revived as a new iOS/iPadOS app!

It's a casual puzzle game you can easily enjoy anytime, anywhere, with simple controls: just tap to clear the wobbly, jiggly jelly blocks. Test your thinking skills with gameplay that's simple yet profoundly deep.

Game Features

Easy but Deep! A Simple-Operation Puzzle Game

  • Just tap two or more connected jelly blocks of the same color to clear them!
  • When blocks disappear, new jellies fall from above, creating chances for combos.
  • Strategize where to clear blocks to aim for high scores!
  • Look out for the appearance of Rainbow Blocks and items (Delete, Change, etc.)!
  • Compete with players worldwide for scores using the ranking feature!

Cute, Wobbly Jelly Blocks!

  • The jelly texture is so well-expressed, it's fun just to look at and makes you want to touch them.
  • The exhilarating animations and sounds when you clear blocks are truly addictive!

Nostalgia and New Discoveries

  • For fans of "Petit Same Puzzle," that unique puzzle experience you remember is faithfully recreated.
  • Of course, even first-time players will quickly find themselves hooked by its addictive nature.

Play Easily Anytime, Anywhere

  • Perfect for your commute, school time, or short breaks – ideal for filling those small gaps in your day.
  • You can play offline, so you can enjoy it even in places without a signal.

【Future Update Plans】

  • We plan to add various game modes, including Classic Mode and Countdown Mode!
