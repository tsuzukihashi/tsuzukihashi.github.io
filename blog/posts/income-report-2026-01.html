分析Analysis

94,209円、前月から15.2%伸びました。10万円まであと少し。

今月の出来事は2つあります。ひとつはGoogle Playの初売上102円。金額は小さくても、iOS一本足だった収益にAndroidという2本目の足が生えました。

もうひとつは、先月仕込んだ買い切りプランが英語圏で動き出したことです。Medicine Reminderで3件、13,272円。サブスクに疲れたユーザーは確実にいて、海外では買い切りの訴求がよく刺さる。この学びは後々効いてきます。

$612, up 15.2% from last month. Almost at $649.

Two things happened this month. One was Google Play's first sale, $0.66. Small as it is, my revenue had been standing on iOS alone, and now it has a second leg in Android.

The other is that the one-time purchase plans I set up last month started moving in English-speaking markets. Medicine Reminder sold 3, for $86. There are definitely users who are tired of subscriptions, and abroad, a one-time purchase lands well. This lesson pays off later.

Revenue starting to spread across five apps is a good sign, too.

数値について。AdMobは管理画面の推定収益額で、確定額とは数％ずれることがあります。App Storeは手数料を引いた開発者受取額で、外貨分は月末レートで円に換算した概算を含みます。Google Playはサービス手数料を引いた受取額です。3つとも暦月の1日から末日で区切っています。App Store Connectの財務報告はAppleの会計月で締めるので、同じ月でも対象期間がずれ、画面に出る金額とは一致しません。締め日の関係で、実際の振込額とも一致しません。
About the numbers. AdMob figures are the estimated earnings shown in the dashboard and can differ from the finalized amount by a few percent. App Store figures are developer proceeds after Apple's commission, and include estimates where foreign-currency sales were converted to yen at the month-end rate. Google Play figures are proceeds after the service fee. All three are cut by calendar month, from the 1st to the last day. App Store Connect's financial reports close on Apple's fiscal months, so the period covered shifts even for the same month, and the amounts won't match what's shown there. Because of payment cutoff dates, they won't match the actual payouts either. Dollar amounts are converted from yen at the ECB month-end rate (¥154.03 per US dollar on 2026-01-30), and every figure in this issue, including last month's, uses that same rate.
