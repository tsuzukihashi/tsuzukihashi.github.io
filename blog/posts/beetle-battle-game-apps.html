僕が作ったアプリ

昆虫大戦

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

グー、チョキ、パーのそれぞれに技カードを置いておき、じゃんけんで勝った手の技が出ます。どの手にどの技を置くかが、そのまま作戦になります。

あいこが続くと、お互いに角を押し合ってダメージが大きくなります。手を選ぶ10秒のあいだも、2匹は3Dで押し合っています。

ヘラクレスオオカブトやギラファノコギリクワガタなど、実在する20種が入っています。図鑑で学名や体長、分布も読めて、同じ種でも1匹ずつ体長が違います。

買い切りで、広告もアプリ内課金もアカウント登録もありません。近くの人と、その場で対戦もできます。

虫＆バトル

Morimirai Inc. · App Storeの評価 897件

評価が897件あって、この中ではいちばん遊ばれています。虫をデフォルメせず、実際の姿で描いているのが特徴です。

カブトムシやクワガタだけでなく、チョウやアリ、ハチも出てきます。チームを組むターン制のバトルで、ほかのプレイヤーが育てたチームとも戦えます。カブトムシは、ストーリーの3章まで進めると仲間になります。

超虫相撲

RYOU TANAKA · App Storeの評価 47件

木をよく観察して虫を捕まえ、特訓とバトルで育てるゲームです。同じ種類でも、力やすばやさが1匹ずつ違います。

バトルは2Dの虫相撲で、相手を倒すか土俵の外に押し出せば勝ちです。ビームを放つ虫や巨大化する虫など、派手な必殺技があります。

カブトムシ観察&バトル日記

Sumie Watanabe · App Storeの評価 151件

御神木の樹液に集まるカブトムシを観察するゲームです。ゼリーを置いたり幼虫を育てたりして、大きなカブトムシを目指します。

全部の機能が無料で、バナー広告が出ると説明文に書いてあります。戦わせるより、育てて眺めたい人向けですね。

よくある質問

無料で遊べますか？
虫＆バトル、超虫相撲、カブトムシ観察&バトル日記は無料で入れられます。昆虫大戦は最初に買う買い切りで、そのあとの課金はありません。
子どもでもルールが分かりますか？
昆虫大戦の基本はじゃんけんです。勝った手に置いた技が出るだけなので、ルールはすぐ覚えられます。
iPadでも遊べますか？
4本ともiPadに対応しています。昆虫大戦は、近くの人との対戦もiPhoneとiPadでできます。

昆虫大戦はまだ評価が0件なので、どの甲虫で勝ったか聞かせてもらえたらうれしいです🙏

Beetle Wars

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

You set a skill card on each of rock, paper, and scissors, and the winning hand fires its skill. Which card goes on which hand is your whole strategy.

If you keep tying, the two beetles shove horns and the damage climbs. Even during the 10 seconds you take to choose a hand, they keep pushing against each other in 3D.

There are 20 real species, including the Hercules beetle and the giraffe stag beetle. The field guide lists scientific names, body lengths, and where they live, and every individual has its own body length.

It's pay-once, with no ads, no in-app purchases, and no account. You can also battle someone nearby, right there.

Bugs & Battle

Morimirai Inc. · 897 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

With 897 ratings, this is the most played of the four. The bugs are drawn as they really look, not as cute characters.

Besides beetles, butterflies, ants, and bees show up too. Battles are turn-based with teams, and you can face teams other players have raised. You get the rhinoceros beetle when you reach chapter 3 of the story.

Super Bug Sumo

RYOU TANAKA · 47 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

You catch bugs by watching trees closely, then raise them through training and battles. Even within one species, each bug has its own strength and speed.

Battles are 2D bug sumo. You win by knocking your opponent down or pushing it out of the ring. There are flashy special moves, like bugs that fire beams or grow huge.

KABUTO Beetle Diary & Battle

Sumie Watanabe · 151 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This is a game about watching rhinoceros beetles gather at the sap of a sacred tree. You put out jelly and raise larvae, aiming for a big beetle.

The description says every feature is free and banner ads are shown. It suits people who would rather raise and watch beetles than fight with them.

Which one to pick

  • If you want to build teams of many kinds of bugs and fight other players, Bugs & Battle
  • If you want sumo with flashy special moves, Super Bug Sumo
  • If you would rather raise and watch than fight, KABUTO Beetle Diary
  • If you want real beetles fighting in 3D with no ads or purchases, Beetle Wars

FAQ

Are they free?
Bugs & Battle, Super Bug Sumo, and KABUTO Beetle Diary are free to download. Beetle Wars is a one-time purchase with nothing more to pay after that.
Can kids understand the rules?
Beetle Wars is built on rock-paper-scissors. The winning hand just fires the skill you put on it, so the rules are quick to learn.
Can I play on iPad?
All four support iPad. In Beetle Wars, you can also battle someone nearby on iPhone or iPad.
