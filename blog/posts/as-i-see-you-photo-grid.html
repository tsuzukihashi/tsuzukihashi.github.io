どんな画像なのか

私から見た友達や私から見た彼氏は、SNSで流行っている3×3の画像です。この人を動物にたとえると、場所にたとえると、のように9つのお題を並べて、それぞれのマスを写真で埋めます。

できた画像をストーリーやLINEで本人に見せるところまでが、だいたいの流れですね。

動物、場所、植物、キャラクター、季節、趣味、食べ物、色、飲み物の9つのお題が最初から入っています。マスを押して、カメラロールの写真を選んでいくだけです。Pinterestなどで見つけた画像も、写真アプリに保存すれば選べます。

写真はピンチで大きさを、ドラッグで位置を合わせられます。色のマスは写真ではなく一色で塗れて、写っている人や物だけを切り抜いて白地に乗せることもできます。切り抜いても元の写真は残るので、いつでも戻せます。

真ん中の見出しは、私から見たパパや私から見た推しのように選ぶか、自分で書けます。お題も自分で決めてセットにしておけて、そのセットをQRコードで友だちに渡すこともできます。渡るのはお題と色だけで、写真は入りません。

書き出すと、できあがりの画像と、空のお題だけの画像の2枚が出ます。空のマスは黒地にお題の文字が入るので、作りかけのままでも1枚の絵になります。

会員登録はなく、写真とボードは端末の中だけに保存されます。無料で、広告が出ます。iPhoneはiOS 18以降、Google PlayのAndroid版はAndroid 10以降で使えます。

アプリを使わずに作るなら

お題の文字が入った3×3のテンプレートの画像を用意して、写真を1枚ずつ重ねていけば作れます。SNSのストーリーの編集画面や、写真を重ねられる編集アプリが使えます。

ただ、9枚の写真をマスにぴったり合わせるのは、思ったより手間がかかります。マスごとに写真を入れるだけで済むのが、専用のアプリのいいところですね。

Short answer

An as-I-see-you image describes someone through nine prompts, like an animal, a place, or a food, and fills a 3×3 grid with photos. What the image is

As-I-see-you images, like ones for a friend or a boyfriend, are 3×3 grids that have been going around on social media. You set out nine prompts, like what animal or what place this person would be, and fill each square with a photo.

Usually the last step is showing the finished image to the person, in a story or on LINE.

Making it with the app

I made this

As I See You

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store Android version on Google Play

It comes with nine prompts built in: animal, place, plant, character, season, hobby, food, color, and drink. Just tap a square and pick a photo from your camera roll. Images you found on Pinterest or elsewhere work too, once you've saved them to Photos.

Pinch to resize a photo and drag to move it. The color square can be filled with a single color instead of a photo, and you can cut out just the person or thing in a picture and place it on white. The original stays, so you can always switch back.

You can pick the heading in the middle, like Dad as I see him or my favorite idol as I see them, or write your own. You can also set your own prompts and save them as a set, then pass that set to a friend with a QR code. Only the prompts and colors go across, never your photos.

When you export, you get two images, the finished grid and a blank one with only the prompts. Empty squares show the prompt on black, so even an unfinished grid looks complete.

There's no sign-up, and your photos and boards stay on your device. It's free with ads. It runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later, and the Android version on Google Play runs on Android 10 or later.

Making it without the app

You can also start with a 3×3 template image that already has the prompts on it, and layer your photos onto it one by one. A story editor on social media or any photo editor that can stack images will do.

The catch is that fitting nine photos neatly into the squares takes more work than you'd expect. Being able to just drop a photo into each square is what a dedicated app is good for.

FAQ

Is it free?
Yes, it's free. It shows ads instead.
Are my photos sent anywhere?
Your photos and boards are saved only on your device and never sent to the developer's servers. When you share prompts with a QR code, no photos are included.
Can I change the prompts?
Yes. You can choose all nine prompts yourself and save them as sets, like ones for your favorite idol or for family, to use again and again.
Can I make one on Android?
Yes. There's an Android version on Google Play that runs on Android 10 or later.
