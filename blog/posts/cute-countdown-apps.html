Himekuri｜記念日カウント・あと何日・付き合って何日目

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

推しのライブ、旅行、受験のような楽しみな日を入れると、毎日その日まで数えてくれます。付き合った日から何日目かを数えるのにも使えます。

見た目はコーラル、さくら、よぞら、まっちゃのような12種類のテーマから選びます。カードとウィジェットが同じ色になります。当日は紙吹雪でお祝いします。

無料だと登録は3件まで、テーマは3種類で、通知は当日の朝9時だけです。毎年や毎月の繰り返し、7日前と前日の通知、残りのテーマはプレミアムで、買い切りか週額で選べます。会員登録はなく、データは端末の中に保存されます。

無料で使えますか？
4本とも無料で入れられます。Himekuriは3件まで無料で、あと何日はイベントをいくつでも登録できると書かれています。
ロック画面に残り日数を出せますか？
Himekuri、あと何日、TheDayBeforeの3本がロック画面のウィジェットに対応しています。
付き合って何日目かも数えられますか？
Himekuri、あと何日、カウントするおは、過去の日付から何日目かを数えられます。TheDayBeforeは100日や1周年の通知が自動で入ると書かれています。毎年の記念日として繰り返すのは、Himekuriではプレミアムの機能です。

Himekuri - Day Countdown

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Add something you're looking forward to, like a concert, a trip, or an exam, and it counts down every day. It can also count the days since you started dating.

Pick from 12 themes like coral, cherry blossom, night sky, and matcha. Your cards and widgets change to match. On the day itself, confetti falls to celebrate.

The free version holds 3 dates and 3 themes, with a single reminder at 9 a.m. on the day. Yearly and monthly repeats, reminders 7 days and 1 day before, and the rest of the themes are Premium, available as a one-time purchase or weekly. There's no account, and your data stays on your phone.

Countdown & Count Up Timer

Yoji Shimobayashi · 14,486 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

With 14,486 ratings, it's by far the most used of the four. You can add as many events as you like, and choose whether to count in years, months, weeks, days, or hours.

Its strength is using your own photo as the background, so you can build a countdown around a photo of your favorite artist or your partner. It notifies you of the days left at a set time each day and backs up to iCloud.

TheDayBefore (days countdown)

TheDayBefore, Inc. · 820 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

This one gives you the most to decorate with. Background images, icons, stickers, and fonts, and the widget shows it all just the way you set it.

Reminders can be set for the day itself, a week, 3 days, or 1 day before, and 100-day and one-year milestones are added automatically. There's also a feature where people waiting for the same day can comment together.

Count Suruo - days count

mitsuhiro nara · 3,530 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

It has themes like cats, bears, and pandas, which suits animal lovers. All your counts sit together in one list.

You can show one count as a live timer that ticks from years down to seconds. There's a widget too. The description doesn't say whether it sends reminders.

Which one to pick

  • If you want your favorite photo as the background and lots of events, Countdown & Count Up Timer
  • If you want to go all in with stickers and fonts, TheDayBefore
  • If you like animal themes, Count Suruo
  • If you want it cute just by picking a color theme, with no sign-up, Himekuri

FAQ

Are they free?
All four are free to download. Himekuri is free for up to 3 dates, and Countdown & Count Up Timer says you can add as many events as you like.
Can I show the days left on my Lock Screen?
Himekuri, Countdown & Count Up Timer, and TheDayBefore all have Lock Screen widgets.
Can they count how long we've been together?
Himekuri, Countdown & Count Up Timer, and Count Suruo can count the days since a past date. TheDayBefore says it adds 100-day and one-year reminders automatically. In Himekuri, repeating a date every year is a Premium feature.
