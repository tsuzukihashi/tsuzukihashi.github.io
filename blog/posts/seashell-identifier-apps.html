ShellLens - 貝殻AI図鑑

Ryo Tsudukihashi · App Storeの評価 0件

貝殻を撮るか、写真ライブラリから選ぶと、AIが名前の候補を出します。和名と英名、学名、分類、大きさ、生息地、特徴までは無料で見られます。

いちばん近い候補のほかに、似ている候補も並びます。違うと思ったら名前を自分で直せます。調べた貝殻はコレクションにたまっていきます。

無料で調べられるのは2回までです。何回でも調べたいときや、食性などの生態、レア度や価値の目安を見たいときはプレミアムです。調べるときはネットにつながっている必要があります。

名前と学名のほかに、よく似た種類との違いも学べます。情報がある貝ならWikipediaの記事や、海の生き物のデータベースの分類や分布も出ます。

結果が違うと思ったら、考えている名前を入れるとAIが同じ写真を見直します。集めた貝殻は海岸や旅行ごとのフォルダに分けて、メモも付けられます。週額か年額のサブスクリプションがあります。

ShellLens - Seashell AI Guide

Ryo Tsudukihashi · 0 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Take a photo of a shell or pick one from your library, and the AI suggests what it is. The Japanese and English names, scientific name, classification, size, habitat, and features are all free to see.

Besides the closest match, it lists other likely candidates. If you think the answer is wrong, you can correct the name yourself. Every shell you look up goes into your collection.

You can identify two shells for free. Unlimited identifications, plus ecology details like diet and an estimate of rarity and value, come with Premium. You need an internet connection to identify a shell.

Sea Shell Identifier - AI ID

Bohdan Hordiienko · 39 App Store ratings

View on the App Store

Its Japanese name also includes the word ShellLens, but it's a different developer's app, not mine. With 39 ratings, it has the most of the three.

Along with the name and scientific name, it explains how a shell differs from similar species. When the data exists, it also shows the Wikipedia article and the classification and recorded range from a marine species database.

If you think a result is off, type the name you have in mind and the AI takes another look at the same photo. You can sort your shells into folders by beach or trip and add notes. It offers weekly and yearly subscriptions.

Shell Identifier: Rarity Value

BASAK GEDIK · 1 App Store rating

View on the App Store

Besides the name, it shows how rare a shell is and an estimated price. The description says the price is based on online market data.

You can save each find with its photo, place, and date. The description doesn't say what is free and what isn't.

Which one to pick

  • If you want to learn how a shell differs from look-alikes, or sort finds by beach, Sea Shell Identifier
  • If you mostly wonder how rare your shell is, Shell Identifier: Rarity Value
  • If you first want to see the name, scientific name, and habitat for free, ShellLens

FAQ

Are they free?
All three are free to download. ShellLens lets you identify two shells for free, and after that it's Premium. The other two don't say in their descriptions how much is free.
Can I trust the AI answer?
Every one of these apps gives you candidates, not a final answer. Sea Shell Identifier's own description says results can be wrong. For a shell you really care about, compare a few of the candidates.
Can they tell me what a shell is worth?
ShellLens Premium and Shell Identifier: Rarity Value both show an estimate. If you're actually buying or selling, ask a specialist shop or someone who knows shells.
Do they work on a beach with a weak signal?
ShellLens needs the internet to identify a shell. Sea Shell Identifier also needs a connection for new identifications, but says saved results can be viewed offline.

ShellLens doesn't have a single rating yet, so I'd love to hear from anyone who tries it on a shell they found 🐚
