Mission

誰も作らないものを、まじめにMaking what nobody else will, seriously

うんちを記録するアプリも、目薬をさす時間を知らせるアプリも、貝殻を判定する図鑑も、
どこかの誰かが「市場がない」と切り捨てたものです。その切り捨てられた側を拾って、手を抜かずに作る。An app for logging your poop, an app that tells you when to use your eye drops, a field guide that identifies seashells.
Each of them is something someone, somewhere wrote off with "there's no market for that." I pick up what got written off and build it without cutting corners.

“こんなの誰が使うの”が、
毎日使われている。“Who would ever use this?”
Turns out, people use it every day.

うんちくんは21万人が入れました。猫背リマインダーに3,640人、目薬リマインダーに623人。 数の大小ではなく、誰かの一日に居座れたかどうかで見ています。210,000 people have installed UNCHIKUN. 3,640 installed Posture Reminder, and 623 installed Eye Drop Reminder. I don't judge an app by how big the number is. I judge it by whether it earned a spot in someone's day.
